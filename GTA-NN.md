# Grand Theft Auto: Нижний Сити

A 3D open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA: Vice City, moved to Nizhny Novgorod in the summer of 2000. Built from scratch for this project: WebGL2 renderer, Canvas 2D HUD, WebAudio synthesis, all modules under `globalThis.GtaNN` in `js/apps/gtann/nn-*.js`. The city is the real Avtozavodsky district at 1:1 scale, rebuilt from OpenStreetMap and checked against a 2004 satellite image, with ten story missions (staged cutscenes, voiced lines, checkpoints and a retry menu), the metro (four stations of the Avtozavodskaya line with trains, passengers and a 5-rouble fare), the taxi and militia side jobs, 29 vehicle types (cars, trucks, buses and motorcycles of the time, militia, OMON and army vehicles, a БТР-80 and a Т-72Б the player can steal), 17 weapons and the Avtozavodsky park with a Ferris wheel, a shooting gallery and shawarma stalls. Other districts are added later as further entries of `GtaNN.DISTRICTS`.

## One page

- **Fantasy.** A man who sat in prison from 1992 to 2000 walks out into a country he does not recognise: kiosks, pagers, foreign cars, dollars in exchange booths. He has to find stolen money in a city where everyone is somebody's friend.
- **Aesthetic (MDA).** Fantasy and sensation first (driving a Volga through Sotsgorod at sunset with the radio on), challenge second (missions), discovery third (stashes, jumps, side jobs).
- **Core verbs.** Drive, steal a car, run, fight, shoot, throw, lose the militia.
- **Loops.** Moment: driving and shooting feel good without a goal, and the street answers back (benches, fences, bins, signs and posts break and fly with their own weight). Session: a mission or a side job with a clear start marker, a rising middle and a payout. Meta: money buys weapons, clothes and property; story missions open new contacts and the ending of the chapter; the camera (F8) keeps the summer of 2000 as files on the desktop and sells twelve views of the district to the newspaper «Автозаводец».
- **Pillars.** «Лето 2000 года» (every sign, car and line belongs to that summer), «Город живёт сам» (traffic, pedestrians, militia and radio work without the player), «Каждая миссия — новый глагол» (each mission teaches or twists one verb).
- **Out of scope for this release.** Other districts (Leninsky, Kanavinsky and the upper town come next), boats, aircraft, interiors beyond the mission rooms, multiplayer.

## The city

The map is the Avtozavodsky district of Nizhny Novgorod as it stood around 2000–2004, at real scale. Coordinates are metres in a local east-north frame centred on 56.24° N, 43.86° E: x points east, z points south. The play area is the district between Щербинки, Соцгород and the Oka, with a tongue in the north-east up to the Мызинский мост. Roads that leave it end at road-works barriers with ГИБДД posts; the Oka forms the south-east edge.

### Data

`js/apps/gtann/nn-avto.js` holds the district as compact strings (decimetre deltas in base 36): the road network with lanes, one-way and median flags, yard roads and car-park aisles, footpaths, 7 500 building footprints with levels and roof shapes, land use, the Oka with its banks and islands, railways and tram tracks, fences, points of interest (traffic signals, stops, metro entrances, monuments, chimneys, gates) and named neighbourhoods. The source is OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Everything built after about 2005 was removed by hand against the 2004 satellite image on retromap.ru (map 1420044): new residential complexes, towers of 17 floors and more, churches, malls, the «Водный мир» water park (it appears as a construction site) and the sand fill of the «Юг» district. The import scripts live outside the repository; the generated file is the only artefact.

`N.DISTRICTS` is a list, so another district is one more data file registered before `nn-map.js`, in the same frame.

### Runtime modules

- `nn-map.js` decodes the data and builds the drivable network: junction polygons with curved kerbs from the kerb-line intersections, lanes as offset polylines, Bézier connectors, traffic lights from the real signal positions (grouped by crossing, two phases by axis), the sidewalk graph, a 2 m ground raster for surface lookups, spatial hashes, routes.
  - Compound junctions: OSM often splits one signalled crossing into two to four nodes joined by short links. A link shorter than 25 m between two signal nodes of the same group is `e.inner` (`compounds()`): it has no sidewalks, no markings, no zebra and no traffic light, AI cars on it do not stop for the light, and it is drawn untrimmed; when three or more nodes are linked, the hull of their centres (`city.fills`) is paved too, so no kerb islands or raster gaps are left in the middle of the crossing.
  - Zebras at junctions come from the mapped OSM crossings (`zebras()` sets `en.zebra` on an arm when a crossing point lies within the stop-line window of that arm). A signal group without any mapped crossing gets zebras on the arms that leave the group. «Пешеходный переход» signs and crossing railings follow `en.zebra`. Mid-block zebras still come from the crossing points between junctions.
  - Trolleybus wires (`wires()` in `nn-geo.js`) hang over the kerb lane of each trolley edge and stop at the junction edge like the lanes do. Inside a junction `GtaNN.wireLinks` joins them: the straightest partner when one exists, otherwise every turn, then every wire end that is still loose gets its best partner (a U-turn loop at a terminus); `GtaNN.wireCurve` draws the joins as the same cubic curves as the lane connectors.
  - Trees from `greenery()` keep 0.9 m clear of foot paths, yard drives and door paths, and never stand where `surfaceAt` is road or sidewalk.
- `nn-town.js` turns footprints into buildings (style by type, floors and era), barriers from fences, street furniture (lamps, lights, stops with kiosks, metro pavilions, monuments, phones, playgrounds, billboards), 45 000 trees, parking spots, pedestrian areas and the play-area boundary.
- `nn-green.js` (after `nn-street.js`) plants everything soft and the boulders, stumps and logs (`City.wilds()`, see «Vegetation and ground cover»); `nn-flora.js` (after `nn-detail.js`) builds and streams the plant meshes and the grass.
- `nn-crowd.js` (after `nn-ai.js`) extends the walk graph with footpaths, yard drives and podezd doors, builds the footfall grids and runs the passers-by (see «Passers-by»).
- `nn-street.js` places the breakable street kit (bin sites, road signs, crossing railings, picket fences, fruit crates, barrels), cuts metal fences into panels and builds the breakable registry `city.brks`; `nn-debris.js` simulates what breaks (see «Breakable street furniture»).
- `nn-sites.js` places the story locations on real buildings and adds the landmarks (see «District landmarks»). Every mission place has a `yaw` and a frame rotation `rot`; mission scripts write offsets in the place frame and `GtaNN.placeAt(place, dx, dz)` turns them into world coordinates.
- `nn-models.js` builds cars (front doors and their windows are separate meshes hinged at the front edge, with seats, dashboard and a steering wheel inside), pedestrians and pickups; `nn-anim.js` poses the pedestrians every frame (see «Characters and animation»).
- `nn-guns.js` builds the weapons and their moving parts, holds the grip data (`N.WGRIP`), adds the weapon effects to `FX` (cases, back-blast, rocket smoke, the Molotov burst, flames, steam) and draws rockets, thrown bottles and grenades, fire pools and burning people (`N.Guns.draw`, see «Weapons»).
- `nn-metro.js` (simulation, after `nn-ai.js`) holds the metro: station data, the layout under the interior space (`City.metroLayout`, `GtaNN.MetroLine`), the timetable and the trains, riding, turnstiles, passengers and militia (`GtaNN.Metro`, `world.metro`); `nn-metrogfx.js` (after `nn-detail.js`) builds and draws it (see «Metro»).
- `nn-cars.js` builds every vehicle after its real prototype (see «Car bodies»); `GtaNN.Models.car(model, livery, lod)` calls `GtaNN.Cars.build` and caches the result. `nn-models.js` keeps the shared car kit (`GtaNN.CarKit`: lamps, plates, wear) and the generic wheel.
- `nn-army.js` builds the armour, the БТР-80 and the Т-72Б, with separate turret and gun meshes (see «Army vehicles»); it loads after `nn-cars.js`.
- `nn-landmarks.js` builds the landmark buildings that have their own model (`GtaNN.Landmarks`, picked by `building.lm.type`), the park gate, the GAZ gate, the fountain, the mosaic stands, statues and the Lenin and bust monuments; `nn-detail.js` builds the near detail of ordinary houses per chunk while the camera is close (see «Building detail»).
- `nn-geo.js` builds the static geometry in 128 m chunks. Ground is the land polygon north-west of the left bank (with the river cut out); land use, yards, roads, junctions and markings are ground decals drawn after the opaque pass without depth writes; sidewalks, kerbs, buildings, fences and props are real geometry. Building walls get a whole number of window modules per wall, a plinth, parapets or cornices, shopfronts on Stalin-era houses facing main streets, pitched, hipped and saw-tooth roofs. The right bank, the far land and the Мызинский мост are a backdrop mesh.

### Building detail

Facades are made of whole floors: `GtaNN.floorGrid(height, fh)` divides the wall above the plinth (or above the shop floor) into a whole number of floors and stretches the floor height by up to 25 %, so every facade ends with a whole row of windows under the cornice and starts with a whole row over the plinth. Only very low sheds that cannot fit a stretched floor keep a cut, and it is at the bottom. `geo.facadeGrid(b, st, e)` returns that grid (`mods`, `us`, `yb`, `shop`, `floors`, `fh`, `base`) and is shared by the wall code, the balconies and the other near detail. Landmark faces use the same rule (`fit` in `face` and `bays`).

Roof machine rooms on the nine- and sixteen-storey houses stand over the stairwells: `geo.roofBoxSpots(b)` puts one behind each entrance (`geo.doorsOf(b)`) and keeps only boxes that lie wholly inside the roof outline; houses without entrances fall back to boxes along the axis, again only inside the outline. Each box has a steel door with a concrete step, a vent grille and a cap, some carry an antenna.

Entrances: `City.addDoor` puts every `podezd` door in the middle of a facade module of its wall, so the stairwell above it lines up with the window grid. `City.doorFurniture` (run after the sites) lays an asphalt path from each door to the nearest yard road, footpath or sidewalk (`city.doorPaths`, up to 18 m, drawn as ground decals), and puts a bench and a litter bin (prop `urn`) by about two doors in five; doors that open against a neighbouring wall get no path.

Ordinary houses keep their cheap chunk geometry; the extra detail is built per chunk only near the camera (`renderer.updateDetail`: chunks closer than `GtaNN.CITY_DETAIL[preset].r` are built at up to 3 ms a frame and freed 64 m further out; a chunk takes 0.28 ms on average, 5.5 ms at most). `GtaNN.Detail` adds, from the same facade grid the walls use (`geo.facadeGrid`):

- stairwells on the panel and brick houses: above every entrance the module column is covered with plain wall from the window-free band of the facade texture (`STAIR[style].band`, stacked so the bricks keep their size) and gets smaller windows half a floor up from the flats' windows, from just over the door canopy to the last whole window under the roof;
- balconies by style (`BAL`): paired balconies with panel fronts on the khrushchyovkas, loggias on the nine-storey panel houses, bars on brick and tower blocks, stone balustrades on Stalin-era fronts facing a street, a few on the Sotsgorod blocks; some are glazed in brown frames, some carry laundry or a satellite dish; balconies and air conditioners stay out of the stairwell columns and off the walls with entrances where the style says so;
- basement vents in the plinth, bars on some ground-floor windows of the five-storey houses, metal flashing on the parapets;
- on the flat roofs of the five-storey houses: roof hatches over the stairwells and brick vent stacks along the ridge;
- drain pipes, roof antennas standing on the roof slab (one to three cross bars, some with a thin guy wire), air conditioners on the panel towers;
- through passages in the middle of long Sotsgorod blocks.

Ground-floor shops: `City.shopFronts` gives houses on main streets one or more shops (`building.shopSegs`) with a sign from `GtaNN.SHOPS`, and the facade gets shop windows, an awning and a step there. Stalin-era fronts keep their full shop floor.

Overhead lines: trolleybus streets (`edge.trolley`) get two pairs of contact wires and span wires between the street lamps opposite each other (`city.spans`; the lamps are not staggered there); tram lines get a contact wire and poles with brackets beside the track (`city.tramPoles`, colliders), never on the carriageway.

Settings → «Детализация города» (`settings.city`): «высокая» (detail within 230 m, full trees and bushes within 170 m, grass within 70 m), «средняя» (150 m, 120 m, 45 m), «низкая» (no detail, far trees only, no grass; `GtaNN.CITY_DETAIL`, `GtaNN.FLORA_DETAIL`).

### Signs

All lettering (shop and wall signs, road signs, roof letters, neon, reliefs, billboards, number plates, car lettering) lives in one canvas atlas built at load time (`GtaNN.buildAtlas`). The layout is a pure function (`GtaNN.atlasItems` collects one item per `style|text`, `GtaNN.atlasLayout` sizes and packs them, `GtaNN.atlasMap` turns cells into UVs), so the test checks it in node with an estimated text width.

- A text cell has the natural width of its text at the style's font size (`GtaNN.SIGN_STYLE`), never the size of a particular sign. If the narrowest sign with that text is too short, the text is first condensed to 80 % and then set smaller.
- Free lettering (`relief`, `paint`, `roof`) is letter-spaced up to half an em to fill its sign; a very long frieze gets a lower cell instead of a squeezed one.
- Cells are packed in shelves by height with 4 px gutters filled from the cell edges, UVs are inset by half a texel. The canvas is 2048 wide and grows from 2048 to 4096 high when the cells need it (today about 2800 px).
- Map entries are `[u0, v0, u1, v1, aspect, cap]`. `GtaNN.signFace(mb, x, y, z, nx, nz, w, h, entry, kind, back, col)` draws a sign facing (nx, nz) so that it always reads left to right from the front: a plate sign wider than its text is drawn as caps, stretched plain strips and the text; free lettering keeps its proportions inside the box; `back` adds a dark back plate, `col` tints the letters. City signs, kiosks, bus stops and the traffic police post all go through it. Roof signs (`roofSign`) read from the front only: behind the letters stand their dark backs and a steel lattice of posts and rails.
- Billboards (`City.billboards`, prop `billboard`) carry a poster with a picture for each advert (pager, beer, elections, mobile phone, ГАЗель, jeans, internet café, loans, «Волга», the GAZ emblem), three lamps on arms that glow at night and a service walkway under the poster; the poster faces the same way as the frame.

## Driving and vehicle physics

Arcade handling in the spirit of the 3D GTA games, built on a small rigid body per vehicle in `nn-phys.js`. The vehicle state is the ground point under the body centre (`x`, `y`, `z`), the attitude as yaw, pitch and roll (the order `GtaNN.M3.euler` draws with) and their rates (`wz`, `wp`, `wr`), and the velocity of the centre of mass (`vx`, `vy`, `vz`). Code elsewhere still sets `x`, `z`, `yaw` and `wz` directly (teleports, lane traffic, explosions), and the next physics step takes them as they are. `World.stepVehicles` runs four sub-steps for the player's vehicle and three for the others; lane traffic is not simulated.

- **Handling data.** `Phys.handling(spec)` derives and caches everything from `GtaNN.VEHICLES` and the per-model overrides in `GtaNN.HANDLING` (`gears`, `soft` for softer springs, `damp`, `stiff` for anti-roll, `tb` for the front share of the grip, `dmg` for crash damage, `engine: 'rear'` for the ЗАЗ). Classes car, high (jeeps and vans), truck (trucks and buses), bike, apc and tank give the spring sag, bump travel, damping, roll and pitch lever arms, tyre peak slip and steering fall-off.
- **Suspension.** Four springs at the wheel corners; their compression comes from `city.groundY` under each wheel, so kerbs, ramps and decks lift the corners one by one. Spring force is the tyre load, so weight moves to the outside wheels in a turn, to the front under braking and to the rear under power. The body rolls to the outside of a turn (about 4° for a «Жигули» at 0.9 g, more for the УАЗ and the ПАЗ), dives under the brakes and squats on take-off; `v.susp` holds the compression per wheel and the wheels are drawn at it (`Phys.wheelLift`), so they stay on the ground while the body moves. Past the bump travel the wheel hits a stop and an impulse takes over.
- **Tyres.** Every wheel has a slip-angle curve with a peak and a softer slide (80 % of the peak) and a friction circle shared with drive and braking, so a rear-wheel-drive car steps out under full power in a low gear and a front-wheel-drive car pushes wide. The handbrake locks the rear wheels into a slide; a short pull and countersteer make a drift, a long pull with full lock swings the car round. The steering lock shrinks with speed to what the front tyres can use, countersteer may go past it while the rear slides, and with no steering input the front wheels follow the direction of travel. A drift assist pulls the yaw towards what the front wheels ask for while the rear slides, so a slide is caught with the keyboard.
- **Engine.** Force falls from the launch value `acc` to zero at `vtop` = 0.93 · `vmax`; the automatic gearbox (`v.gear`, `v.rpm`, 4 or 5 speeds) shifts at the top of each gear with a 0.15 s pause; the engine sound follows `v.rpm`. W and S together at a standstill (`ctl.burn`) hold the car on the brakes and spin the driven wheels.
- **Air and flips.** Eight hull points (bottom corners, roof corners) keep the body out of the ground when the springs are not enough: hard landings, a car on its side or on its roof, a car thrown by a blast. In the air A / D roll the car and the pitch settles towards the flight path. A car on its side is rocked back with A / D; a car left on its roof catches fire after 2.5 s. Pitch past ±90° is rewritten as the equivalent turned-around attitude, so the car can flip end over end. A blast pushes the car at its side nearest to the explosion and up (`Phys.blast`), so it rolls away from the fire.
- **Collisions.** Vehicles and walls exchange impulses at bumper height with friction: a car hit in the side spins and slides away, a car glancing off a fence slides along it with sparks (`scrape` events) instead of stopping dead. Every collision and every hard ground contact leaves the velocity change it caused in `v.hitDv`; `World.crash` turns it into damage once per frame.
- **Damage.** `GtaNN.crashDamage(v, dv)` = 2.6 · (dv − 5)^1.5 · `dmg`: nothing below 5 m/s (a bump at walking pace, a kerb, a normal jump landing), about 9 % of a «семёрка» for a 60 km/h hit into a fence, 30 % at 108 km/h, a head-on at 50 km/h each costs both cars about a tenth. A heavy vehicle hurts a light one more. Smoke starts below 65 % of health (white), thickens below 45 % and turns black below 25 %, where the engine catches fire (`v.fire` 0.5, flames under the bonnet or at the rear of the ЗАЗ) and the car explodes about six seconds later unless repaired; a car brought to zero by a blast explodes at once. Bullets, fire pools and explosions still damage cars as before.
- **Effects.** Skid marks are laid by sliding or spinning wheels (`FX.skids`, ground sprites that fade in 40 s), tyre smoke rises at the slipping wheels, hard landings thump and shake the camera (`land` events), the lane traffic leans and dips with its own speed changes (`Phys.bodyKin`).
- **Tracks.** The tank keeps its own steering: the hull turns on the spot, the tracks do not slide sideways, and the springs rock the hull when it brakes.

## Street life: traffic and people

Lane traffic (`AI.vmodes.lane` in `nn-ai.js`) is kinematic but drives like a car. It no longer snaps to the lane polyline: every frame `laneAhead` joins the rest of the current lane or connector with the chosen connectors and lanes ahead into one path (`ai.next` plus the queue `ai.q`, picked as far ahead as the plan needs). The car steers to a point 3.5–12 m ahead on that path (pure pursuit on a curvature `ai.k` that changes at a limited rate and is capped by the steering lock), so it starts turning before a junction and its heading never jumps. `ai.s` follows the car's real progress along the path. Speed is planned from the path curvature (`curveLimit`, re-run every 0.15 s): heading change over 8 m and over 4 m windows gives a corner speed for 3.4 m/s² of side force, and the car brakes for it at 2.4 m/s², so right turns are taken at about 15 km/h and left turns at about 20 km/h. A car that ends up off its line or pointing away from the target slows down. At junctions a car follows cars that merge into the same lane on the shared path (`mergeGap`), and waits before entering a connector while a crossing or touching connector at the same node is occupied, or while a car that is closer to that node is about to enter one (`crossBlocked`, conflict sets built once per connector). Measured over 40 s of traffic: yaw jerk p99 below 1 rad/s², side force p99 about 3.5 m/s² (the snapping model had spikes of 175 rad/s² and 53 m/s²).

Gunfire. A shot by the player, a cop or a gangster, a bullet impact and an explosion call `AI.hearShot`: civilian drivers within 28 m of the gun (10 m of the impact, 55 m of a blast) panic. A slow or blocked car is abandoned (`AI.bailOut`: everyone climbs out and runs from the shooter); otherwise the driver floors it in lane mode for 14–22 s (`ai.panic`: half again the lane speed, faster corners, red lights and yields ignored, horn) and bails out if traffic boxes him in. Bullets in the bodywork (`AI.carShot`) do the same, and gangsters get out and shoot back. Pedestrians near the gun or the impact run (`World.alarm`).

Pulled out of the car. Cops at one or two stars and pedestrians in melee (angry drivers, the owner of a stolen car, gangsters with bats) walk round the car to a free front door (`AI.toDoor`, `World.jackSide`) and start `World.npcJack`: the pedestrian gets the `enter` state the player uses, so the rig opens the door, reaches in and pulls (phases `open`, `jack`; `shove` on a motorcycle). Driving off faster than 3 m/s before the pull cancels it, and the jacker is knocked over if the car hits him. `World.pullOut` puts Tyoma outside the door and plays the climb-out: a cop pull ends in «ПОВЯЗАЛИ», anyone else throws him down (`p.pulled`, 0.42 s later) and keeps fighting; the robbed owner gets back in and drives away. A passenger player is still arrested at the door.

Hit by a car. `World.pedVsCar` takes the speed of the car into the pedestrian (not the closing speed, so walking into a parked car hurts nobody). Below 2.2 m/s the pedestrian is pushed aside and stumbles; above it `World.hitPed` turns him to face or show his back to the car, throws him with 80 % of the car speed plus a push and a side component, or over the bonnet when a car under 1.75 m hits him from the front at more than 8.5 m/s, and above 11 m/s spins him head over heels once in the air (`anim.tumble`, drawn around the hips by `nn-anim`). Damage is (closing speed)^1.55 · 1.1, about 75 at 54 km/h; the car loses a little speed (pedestrian mass 80 kg). Airborne bodies pass over cars; a car that rolls over someone lying in the road hurts him (once per 0.45 s), bumps up and does not shove him along. Bodies keep their flight speed until they land, then slide to a stop. Pedestrians watch cars that are not in lane mode (`AI.watchCars`, every 0.1 s): if a car will pass within its half width plus 1 m in under 1.3 s, they step aside or, when it is close, dive (80 % if they face it, 50 % if not, less when it is very close); the others freeze.

Hit by Tyoma's car. `AI.crashReact` runs once per crash (`v.mood`, 8 s), and only when Tyoma's car was moving into the other car before the impact (`v.pvx/pvz`, the velocity at the start of `stepVehicles`), so a car that drives into a parked player does not count. The roll depends on the driver: aggressive share 0.12 + 0.5 · `tough` (less for old people and women), flee 0.26, look 0.22 (0.1 after a light bump), the rest stays in the car. Over 40 rear-end crashes about 30 % of drivers get out. Reactions (`AI.mood`):

- `stay`: brake, hazard lights, horn, a shout, then back into traffic.
- `look`: the car brakes to a stop (`vmodes.stopout`), the driver opens the door and climbs out (`World.npcExit`, the player's `exit` phases stepped from `AI.step`, so the rig opens and shuts the door; a car still moving faster than 2.5 m/s uses the quick climb-out instead), walks round the car to the dent (`pmodes.inspect`, `AI.toCarSpot`), looks at it for 4–8 s, then walks back to the door and drives on. Passengers stay inside.
- `fight`: stop, climb out, attack on foot (with fists that means pulling Tyoma out of his car).
- `ram` (gangsters always choose ram or fight): `vmodes.ram` chases Tyoma's car for 18–32 s. When the car is behind or too close, the driver first drives off and turns round to the left, then charges with a lead on the target; after each hit it backs off for 1.1–1.6 s and charges again, and it backs out when it is stuck. After two rams on a stopped car, or if Tyoma is on foot, the driver gets out and fights. It gives up beyond 160 m or at three stars.
- `flee`: lane driving in panic (`ai.panic`, kept through `AI.bump`), heading away from where Tyoma was (`ai.from`, `rejoin` prefers lanes pointing away from him).

`AI.toCarSpot(w, p, v, lx, lz)` walks a pedestrian to a point next to a car (car-local coordinates): a shortest path over the four corners around the car body, with corners in walls or other cars left out. When the pedestrian has not moved for 1 s, the corner he is stuck at is banned for 5 s. `AI.toDoor` and the pull-outs use it. `World.jackSide` avoids a door that another car presses against.

Blocked lanes. A lane car stops 3.4 m behind something that will not move (`AI.blocks`: a standing pedestrian, a stopped car that is not in lane traffic, or a lane car that is itself going round something) and honks. After 8–12 s (`ai.patience`) `AI.planDetour` tries to go round on the left, then on the right. The side offset is the blocker's rotated half width plus the car's plus 0.55 m. The corridor beside the blocker must be free of walls, poles and cars, and the 45 m beyond it free of oncoming moving cars. If there is not enough room to swing out, the car first backs up to 2 + 1.5 · offset m (`AI.reverseLane`, until something is 0.5 m behind). The detour (`ai.det`, phases `rev`, `out`, `back`) shifts the pursuit path sideways (`ai.off`, 0.6 per metre out, 0.4 back), aims at the shifted path ahead and keeps to 6 m/s. A detour that stays stuck for 6 s is called off. After nine failed plans (about 12 s) the car gives up and turns round: `rejoin` with `turn` looks for a lane in the opposite direction, measured against the heading when the turn began (`ai.tdx/tdz`). A `rejoin` that finds nothing switches to turning after 14 s.

Blowing up inside a car kills everyone in it, the player included, armour or not (only god mode survives).

Tests: section «street reactions» (traffic smoothness, hit at 54 and 18 km/h, parked car, body on the road, dodge share, gunfire panic, bullets in a car, gangsters, the four pull-out cases, crash reactions: mix of moods, share of drivers who get out, animated exits, one roll per crash, ram, flee, look; detour round a parked car after 7–20 s and turning round on a closed road; death in a blown-up car).

## Passers-by

The district should read as a lived-in place: busy where people have a reason to be (stops, shops, the market, the GAZ gate at shift change), quiet in the small hours, with people doing more than walking. The old spawner picked a random sidewalk edge from the whole 330 km network and kept it only inside a 30–95 m ring around the player, so almost every try failed and the street stayed nearly empty at any density setting. `nn-crowd.js` (simulation, after `nn-ai.js`) replaces it.

### Walk network

`City.pathWalk()` (run after `breakables()`) extends the sidewalk graph from `buildWalk()`:

- Every node and edge has a kind `k`: `s` sidewalk, `c` road crossing, `p` footpath or yard drive, `d` door path. Crossings at signalled junctions carry `sig = { n, g }` (junction node and the light group of the crossed arm); sidewalk edges and crossings keep the road edge in `road`.
- OSM footways and steps (`city.foot`) and yard drives and parking aisles (`city.surf`, classes `Y`, `L`) are sampled every 2.5 m and cut where the raster says road or water; nodes every 24 m and at bends, merged when closer than 1 m.
- A loose path end joins the nearest sidewalk edge within 7 m or path edge within 3.5 m (that edge is split at the foot of the perpendicular) when the straight join is clear.
- Every podezd door path (`city.doorPaths`) becomes a door node (`door: true`, `yaw` pointing out) and a short edge to the end of the path, which joins the nearest edge within 22 m with a clear line.
- `pruneWalk()` keeps the largest connected part (about 62 000 nodes: 316 km of sidewalks, 741 km of paths, 4 600 doors). `city.walkOk(e)` checks a path edge once (buildings, fences, play-area walls, water) and the walkers skip blocked ones.
- The taxi job picks its fares and drop-offs from sidewalk nodes only.

### Footfall

`City.crowdIndex()` builds five 40 m grids: home (podezds, playgrounds), transit (bus stops, metro pavilions), shop (OSM shops, kiosks, the market, the univermag), work (the GAZ gate, the port gate, schools and kindergartens, hospitals) and leisure (the ДК, the cinema, the stadium, the ferris wheel, parks). `N.CROWD_DAY` holds an hourly profile per layer plus `street` (`N.crowdHour(layer, h)`): the GAZ gate peaks at 6–8 and 15–17, stops at the rush hours, shops from 9 to 19, leisure in the evening, everything near zero at 2–4. The footfall of an edge is `f0 · street(h) + Σ layer · profile(h)` with `f0` from the street class (main 1, secondary 0.85, tertiary 0.65, residential 0.45; footways 0.35, yard drives 0.22).

### How many and where

`AI.crowd(w)` runs with the traffic every 0.25 s (`AI.populate`). The target is `0.04 · Σ (length · footfall)` over the edges within 100 m, times the «Плотность пешеходов» setting and `wx.crowd` (rain and fog), capped at 75 · setting. At 100 %: 37 near the hotel at 13:00, 47 at 18:00, the cap at the market, 54 at the GAZ gate at noon and 67 at 18:00, 1–5 at 3:00. State is in `world.passers` (`live`, `still`, `target`).

- Up to three new people per tick, 26–96 m away, never in view closer than 72 m. A door spawn may be in view from 10 m: the person comes out of the podezd.
- After a teleport, a metro ride or the start (the player moved more than 60 m in one tick) the ring is filled at once.
- While the player moves faster than 5 m/s, edges ahead are three times as likely and edges behind 0.15 times; hidden people behind are removed beyond 64 m instead of 104 m.
- 30 % of the target is people who stay put (`STILL`): on benches, at stops and kiosks, in chat groups.

### What people do

- `walk`: along the network, one, two (`ai.lead`/`ai.side`, the companion keeps 0.7 m to the side) or three (gopniks); militia patrol in pairs. At a node the next edge is chosen by straightness and footfall, so people drift towards busy places. A door path is taken with the home profile and sets `ai.home`: the person walks into the podezd and disappears, companions with him. Stuck for 3.5 s, they turn round.
- Crossings: at a signalled junction they wait at the kerb until the crossed arm has red and enough red time is left (`min(10, length / 1.7) + 0.5` s); elsewhere they wait for a gap in the traffic within 45 m. After 25 s they go anyway; cars stop for people in the road.
- `bench`: babushkas and pensioners by day, gopniks and muzhiks at night, not in the rain, on podezd and park benches (`p.bench`, the scene draws the bench pose, the sim pins them and skips physics). A bus stop also gets sitters on its own bench.
- `wait`: at bus stops facing the road (count follows the transit profile), in a queue at the kiosk window, near metro pavilions; gopniks squat.
- `chat`: two or three people in a ring on wide sidewalks or in front of a podezd; the speaker changes every 2–6 s and gestures (`anim.talk`), gopniks squat. When the chat ends they walk off together.
- `area`: wandering inside the market, parks and plazas (`city.pedAreas`; the old residential areas are gone, the yard paths replace them).
- `AI.crowdKind` shifts the mix with the hour: babushkas and pensioners by day, students and girls in the day and evening, gopniks, muzhiks and gangsters at night, workers near the GAZ gate at shift change.
- Shots, blasts and hits work as before (`World.alarm`, `AI.flee`): `AI.crowdPre` gets sitters up and stops the gestures when the mode changes.

Cost with 100–120 people around (150 %): scene fill about 2.5 ms and render 1.6 ms per frame, simulation under 1 ms. City build adds about 0.5 s.

F7 «Прохожие» shows how many are near, the target and how many stay put; «Заполнить улицу» fills the ring at once. Tests: section «pedestrians» (network kinds, doors, signals, paths off the carriageway and out of buildings, day profiles, footfall at the market and the GAZ gate, counts at noon, at night, in rain, at 20 % and 150 %, no pop-in, nobody in walls or water, the activity mix, benches, companions, chats, a gunshot, crossing only on red, refill after a teleport, people going in and out of podezds).

### Physics

`nn-debris.js` (`GtaNN.Debris`) simulates the broken pieces as small rigid bodies. A shape is a list of boxes in the prop frame (`BREAK[kind].whole(p)`, `pieces(p)`); from them come the mass, the centre of mass, the full inertia tensor (cylinders for urns, barrels and globes) and the contact points: corners, points along the edges every 0.6 m (thin rods only along two diagonal edges every 0.9 m), and a grid on big faces so that a bumper meets the middle of a fence panel. Each body steps two sub-steps a frame (four when fast or near a car) and solves four sequential-impulse iterations over its contacts with accumulated normal impulses, friction and restitution, then pushes itself out of the deepest contacts.

- **Ground** comes from `city.groundY` per point (cached per frame on a 0.5 m grid), with friction and restitution of the material (`Debris.MAT`).
- **Walls, poles and trees** are the static colliders: a point inside a convex polygon, a box or a circle is pushed out along the nearest side.
- **Cars** are kinematic for debris: a point inside the car's box is pushed out through the nearest face with the car's own velocity (plus its yaw rate), and the opposite impulse slows a simulated car; pieces over 60 kg also feed its crash Δv. Lane traffic pushes pieces aside and does not slow.
- **People** are kicked: light pieces take the walker's speed; fast heavy pieces knock people down and hurt them, credited to whoever broke the thing.
- **Water**: wood floats, metal and concrete sink and are removed on the bed.
- **Sleep**: a body that rests on the ground (or on a standing car) for 0.45 s with almost no motion sleeps until a moving car comes near, a bullet or a blast hits it, or someone kicks it.

Breaking. A car breaks an anchored object when its kinetic energy along the contact normal reaches the anchor energy; the car pays that energy out of its speed and the object becomes a body. Loose objects become bodies at the first touch. A whole body shatters into its pieces when the impulse a car gives it, or a landing, carries more than its `shatter` energy (`J²/2m`); a tank breaks everything into pieces. Tall anchored things (lamps, globe lamps, traffic lights, signs) tip over on a slow hit: their foot is held by a pin for up to 0.7 s or until they lean 35°, they start rotating the way the car went, and the car that hit them does not touch them while pinned, so a post falls ahead of the car instead of flipping back over it. At or above their `fly` speed (`flyOf`: sign 3 m/s, traffic light 5, globe lamp 7, park lamp 6, street lamp 11) they snap off and are thrown instead (`Debris.launch`). The impulse is 1.2 · u · M/(M + m_eff) along the car's path, applied at bumper height (for light things below 60 kg, at 0.75 of the centre of mass height so they fly rather than spin back onto the bonnet), with 30 % sideways when the car hits off-centre and a little lift. The car loses that momentum, and it ignores the body for 0.25 s. A sign hit at 50 km/h flies 15–20 m. Bullets push light things (Δv capped at 3.5 m/s per hit), wear down `hp` (benches, crates, pickets) and spark or splinter by material. Melee hits from the player do the same at arm's length. Explosions dislodge anything whose anchor energy is below `200 · dmg · (1 − d/R)²`, throw it outward and up, and smash pieces when the blast is strong enough.

Dents. Bodies keep up to 10 dents in their own frame (`b.dents`: point, push direction, radius, depth; `Debris.dent`, `impactDent`). Each dent scales with impact speed and material (`Debris.DENT`: metal 1, glass 0.6, concrete 0.2, wood 0.15), and depth is capped at 0.22 m. Sources: the car hit itself (at the bumper, along the car's path, so a struck pole kinks there), hard landings and car contacts later on (the strongest contact of the frame, ground impulse Δv > 6 m/s or car Δv > 5 m/s, at most every 0.25 s), and bullet dings (0.09 m). Nearby dents merge. `N.Props.debris` returns a per-body copy when a body has dents (`N.Props.dentMesh`: triangles near a dent are split to edges of 0.2 m, vertices move by d · (1 − r²/R²)², and moved triangles get flat normals), rebuilt only when `b.dentVer` changes. Cars do not dent yet.

Consequences. Breaking street furniture is not a crime: a sign knocked down in front of a patrol gives no wanted level. A broken record stays broken while the player is near; once it has been broken for 20 s and it and its pieces are more than 110 m from the player, it is put back (`Debris.restore`): the pieces go, the collider is live again and the prop reappears. At most about 220 bodies exist; old sleeping ones away from the player go first.

Tuning knobs are all in `N.BREAK` (mass of the boxes, `anchor`, `shatter`, `hp`, `foot`, `tip`, `fly`), `Debris.MAT` and `Debris.DENT`.

### Drawing and sound

Breakable props are baked into the chunk meshes like every prop; `CityGeo` records each one's vertex range (`geo.brkVerts`). `Scene.syncBroken` compares the world's broken set with what is hidden: a broken prop's range is zeroed (`Renderer.setPropHidden`), a restored one is rebuilt from its builder and written back vertex for vertex, and lamp lights and traffic signal sprites follow (`L.off`). A new game or a loaded save therefore starts with a whole street. Bodies are drawn with `N.Props.debris`: the whole prop mesh, a piece cut from it by assigning every triangle to the smallest piece box that holds its centre, or plain boxes for urn chunks and crate planks; lamp glass is drawn unlit once broken. Events: `pbreak` (break or shatter: material sound — `wood`, `clang`, `crunch`, `rattle` for fences and railings, `glass` — splinters, concrete chips and dust, sparks, glass, fruit, flying rubbish), `phit` (landings and bumps: `knock`, `clank`, `thud`, dust; four per frame at most), `pshot` (bullet hits by material).

## Vegetation and ground cover

Green tells the player where they are and how the ground plays. Three rules decide everything here:

1. **Each place has its own plants.** A yard has lilac and jasmine in the front gardens, flower beds made of painted tyres and the odd tyre swan by the entrances, half-buried tyres or white-washed boulders where the lawn meets the yard drive; avenues have trimmed hedges along the sidewalk and pollarded poplars; the park forest has hazel undergrowth, rowans, ferns, stumps and fallen logs; ponds have reeds, water lilies and willow shrubs; garage rows have burdock, nettle and self-seeded ash-leaved maples; the Oka bank by the debarkader, the river port and the bridge has riprap.
2. **Soft and hard read by silhouette.** Trunks, boulders, stumps and logs stop a car or a person. Leaves (bushes, hedges, reeds, grass) let you through: a car slows, the bush rustles, leaves fly and the plant bends away from it; a person on foot wades slower. Nothing soft ever damages a car.
3. **The budget does not grow.** The static city keeps one cheap far version of every plant; the full plants and the grass exist only near the camera.

### What grows where

Placement is `City.wilds()` in `nn-green.js`, run after the collectibles and before the breakables. Every spot passes `City.greenOk`: inside the play area, soft ground (grass, forest, field; sand and water only for willow shrubs and riprap) at the centre and four points of the radius, `surfaceAt` 3, no claim (`freeAt`), no collider (`clearAt`), and no guard (`City.guarded`: mission places 7 m, jumps and ramps, stashes, pickups, parking spots, door paths, walk-graph edges, OSM foot paths, yard drives, plazas and the footprints of big props). Runs along a road may ignore the guard of segments parallel to them, so a row of boulders can follow a yard drive at 0.8 m from its edge.

### Through the bushes

`World.stepGreen` runs after the vehicles. A moving car (within 140 m of the player) whose centre is inside a soft zone loses `N.GREEN.drag` (0.55/s; hedges 0.9/s, heavy vehicles a quarter) of its velocity and sends `rustle` (at most every 0.45 s per bush): leaf particles in the bush's colours, a rustling sound, and the bend in the shader. A «семёрка» coasting through a lilac bush at about 35 km/h loses a fifth of its speed (22 instead of 29 km/h 1.5 s later). On foot the player wades at 62 % speed and rustles when moving.

Hiding: standing or walking slowly (< 3.2 m/s) inside a bush (not a hedge), not having fired for 2.5 s, sets `world.hidden`; militia then see the player only within 6 m (`N.GREEN.hideR`) and the helicopter not at all, so the wanted level fades as if the player were out of sight. The HUD shows «В КУСТАХ» under the beacons while hidden with a wanted level.

Bullets: `spark` events carry `mat` from the collider kind (`tree`, `stump`, `log` → wood chips and a knock; `rock` → stone chips, a spark and a click).

### Drawing

`CityGeo.build` hands plant props to `GtaNN.Flora` (`nn-flora.js`) instead of `Props.place`:

- **Far LOD** (`tree`, `bush`, `reeds`): two crossed planes and, for trees, a horizontal plane with a painted crown (`leaves_*` layers, drawn from clumps of leaves with light from above) plus two crossed bark quads; 20 vertices a tree, 8 a bush. Every chunk keeps them in its own buffer (`ch.fmb` → `fv`), about 1.15 M vertices for the whole city, as many as the old crossed-plane trees took inside the static chunks.
- **Near** (the same props plus stumps, logs, tyres, flower beds, swans, weeds, lilies): `Flora.nearMesh(p)` builds the full model once per kind and variant (four variants per species) and `floraPut` copies it with the prop's yaw, scale and a per-plant tint. Trees have a tapered, bent trunk with a root flare and limbs, and a crown of crossed leaf cards around an ellipsoid with crown-shaped normals and darker inner and lower leaves (`FLORA_SPEC` forms: broad, pollard, birch with drooping sprays, willow with a curtain, Scots pine with an orange upper trunk and a flat top, spruce in drooping tiers around a painted core); 200–340 vertices. Bushes are stems and 8–13 clumps (110–185 vertices). `renderer.updateFlora` builds the nearest chunk inside `floraR` in slices of 16 plants within a 2.5 ms frame budget (`Flora.nearJob/nearStep`; a forest chunk takes several frames instead of one 20 ms hitch) and frees chunks 48 m beyond; a chunk with its near plants built does not draw its far LOD.
- **Static** (`hedge`, `boulder`, `rubble`): baked into the chunk mesh like other props; boulders are subdivided icosahedra pushed by noise, rubble is flat-shaded concrete with rebar.
- **Grass**: 32 m tiles within `grassR` (`Flora.grass`), 0.6–2.3 tufts per m² by land use (lawn, park, meadow, forest floor with ferns, waste ground with weeds; flowers in a few per cent), only on grass, forest and field cells, never on a road or sidewalk point, inside a house, on a door path, a plaza or under big props. A tuft is two crossed cards with the grass, meadow or weed texture; about 1.2 ms a tile (7 ms at worst), at least one tile per frame.

Textures (in `nn-tex.js`, alpha cut-out with a 20 % alpha backdrop of the mean colour so the mipmaps do not darken): `leafbroad`, `leafsmall`, `leafapple`, `leafrowan`, `leafbloom`, `needles`, `sprucebr`, `hedge`, `grasstuft`, `meadow`, `reeds`, `weeds` (four plants in quadrants), `lily`, `boulder`, `barkpine`, `endgrain`, the far crowns `leaves_birch/linden/poplar/pine/apple/spruce/willow/bush`, and new birch and furrowed bark.

## Camera views

C cycles the view. On foot and in a vehicle the cycles are separate and each is remembered in the settings (`settings.camFoot`, `settings.camCar`), so a player can walk in third person and drive from the cabin. The lists are `GtaNN.CAM_FOOT` (близко, обычная, дальше, сверху, от первого лица) and `GtaNN.CAM_CAR` (the same four, then из салона and с бампера); index `GtaNN.CAM_FP` (4) is first person, `GtaNN.CAM_BUMPER` (5) the bumper. `UI.camMode` is a getter over the two and also takes assignments (the shot tool sets it). The switch prints «Камера: …» at the bottom right for 1.8 s (`msg.view`).

**First person on foot** (`UI.headCamera`). The eye is the head bone of the drawn skeleton: `Scene.addPed` stores the player's eye point (`GtaNN.pedEye`, 0.216 m above the head pivot and 0.13 m in front of it) every frame, and the camera adds the distance the player moved since then, so walking, running and landing move the view with the body. The head is hidden (`GtaNN.headless` zeroes the head bone's rotation, so the head, hair and hat collapse into the neck); the body, arms and weapon stay, and looking down shows them. The body turns with the mouse (`inp.face`): A and D strafe, the legs use the strafe walk. Firearms are always shouldered (`anim.aim` 1): the rig gets `ctx.fp` and puts the weapon low on the right below the eye (hip view); the right button blends it up so the sights sit under the eye and narrows the field of view from 1.15 to 0.8 (`cam.ads`); scoped rifles keep the scope view. Reloads keep the same lift, so the weapon stays in the frame. A white dot marks the aim point (red on a person); with the right button the usual cross is drawn. Every shot lifts the view by `kick[1] × 0.12`. The near plane is 5 cm (`cam.near`), pitch reaches ±75°. In the metro carriage and on the Ferris wheel the seated eye of those places is used as before.

**Cabin** (`UI.cabinCamera`). The eye is the driver's (or passenger's) head from the seated skeleton, in the car frame: the view pitches and rolls with the body (the up vector is 60 % of the car's), the head leans with acceleration and braking (`UI.headSway`: about 5 mm per m/s², at most 6–7 cm) and the steering wheel, the hands on it, the dashboard and the A-pillars are in view. The mouse and Q/E/B look around inside the car and the view returns forward after 1.6 s. Gauges are live: `GtaNN.Cars.inside` returns `mb.dials` (speedometer `spd`, tachometer `rpm`, fixed fuel and temperature needles `fix`; the Volga ribbon speedometer is a growing bar), `Scene.dials` turns a needle mesh over each dial for every car whose interior is drawn (`GtaNN.dialValue`: speed over `GtaNN.dialTop(spec)` km/h, at least 140; revs from `v.rpm`; everything rests at zero without a driver; the needles glow brighter with the headlights on); the painted needles were removed from the `gauge` texture. In rain the near drops, the rain sheets and the lens drops are off inside the cabin (`cam.cabin`). On a motorcycle the view is lower and wider so the bars, mirrors and lamp are in the frame. In a tank or a БТР this view is the commander's head out of the hatch (`UI.hatchCamera`, `shape.hatch` turned with the turret when the hatch is on it); the turret follows the mouse as in the other views.

**Bumper** (`UI.bumperCamera`). On the centre line just ahead of the front bumper, 0.42 m above the clearance; the field of view widens with speed up to +0.24. Looking back (B, or the mouse past 90°) moves it to the rear bumper.

Tests: section «camera views» (cycles and saved settings, eye height and look direction on foot, strafing body, shouldered weapon, cabin eye inside the body under the roof, side look, bumper in front and at the rear, head sway, tank hatch, dials on every car, needle values, first person weapon placement for the АК, ПМ and shotgun, the hidden head).

## Characters and animation

**Models.** A pedestrian is a skeleton of 16 bones (`GtaNN.PB`: pelvis, spine, chest, head, upper arm, forearm and hand on each side, thigh, shin and foot on each side) skinned on the CPU, one bone per vertex. The body is built from smooth tubes, not boxes: the torso is one tube from the crotch to the collar whose rings belong to the pelvis, spine and chest, so it bends without gaps; arms and legs are tubes with a ring on each side of the elbow and knee; the hands are mittens with a thumb; the shoes have a toe and a sole. The head is two half-tubes: the back in skin colour, the front painted from the `face` texture layer, a 4×4 atlas of 16 faces drawn on a canvas (eyes, brows, nose shadow, lips, stubble, moustache, beard, wrinkles, make-up, a scar; `N.faceOf(look)`, `look.face` pins one, Tyoma is face 9, the story characters have their own). Hair is a shell over the skull with a hairline per style (`short`, `long`, `bob`, `bun`, `bald`), hats are shells too (кепка, фуражка with a red band and a badge, каска, платок). Clothes follow `look.topKind`: a jacket opens over a T-shirt, a suit has a white shirt and a tie, the track suit has a zip, a collar and three stripes down the arms and legs, a uniform has a belt, shoulder boards and buttons, a тельняшка-майка leaves the shoulders bare, a dress and a coat have skirts, a girl's top shows the midriff. Patterns are texture layers drawn over the vertex colour (vertex kind 13): `stripes`, `camo`, `palms` (the Hawaiian shirt) and `plaid` (the клетчатая сумка of the челнок). `look.leather` uses the glossy `leather` layer (the leather jacket outfit, bandits, Хромой). Far pedestrians (beyond 42 m) use a lighter model (`N.Models.ped(look, 1)`, about half the vertices, no face).

**Animation** (`nn-anim.js`, `GtaNN.pedRig(p, t, root, piv, ctx)` returns the bone matrices, the weapon matrix and `parts`, a list of `{ mb, m }` for the magazine, pump, barrels, cylinder, feed cover or warhead). Everything is procedural, there are no keyframe files:

- Locomotion blends walk, run and sprint by speed. The simulation advances `anim.phase` at a cadence that grows with the square root of speed (about one stride per second walking, 1.4 running, 1.6 sprinting), so the feet do not slide. Swing legs bend at the knee, the ankle rolls off the toe, the pelvis bobs, sways and turns, the chest counter-rotates, arms swing with the opposite leg and pump when running, the body leans into a sprint. Moving sideways or backwards while aiming steps sideways or backwards. Standing still: breathing, weight shifts from foot to foot, the head looks around, small steps when turning on the spot; old people stoop; idle gopniks squat after a few seconds.
- Weapons are held by inverse kinematics: the weapon frame is placed first, then both arms reach for it. Each weapon's entry in `N.WGRIP` (`nn-guns.js`) names its grip (`pistol`, `rifle` or `wrist` for the shotguns), where the left hand goes (`under` the handguard, `cup` under a pistol, on the `mag` of the Кипарис), how it is aimed (`shoulder` with the stock length, `pistol` at arm's length, `chest` for the Кипарис, `launcher` on the shoulder for the РПГ-7) and how it is carried (across the chest by default, `hip` for the ПКМ, `shoulder` with the tube tilted up for the РПГ-7, `low` for the Кипарис); `N.Anim.hold(id)` gives the hand frames. Aiming turns the body side-on, tilts the head to the stock and follows the aim pitch. Each shot kicks the weapon back and up (`WEAPONS[id].kick`); the shotgun pump goes back and forward after a shot. Pistols are held two-handed when aiming and in one hand otherwise.
- Reloads follow a timeline shared with the sound (`WEAPONS[id].rk`) and are data in `N.Anim.RELOADS`, picked by `WEAPONS[id].reloadAnim`: a weapon pose, left-hand keys on named points of the weapon (points on a moving part follow it, `WGRIP[id].moves`), magazine modes (`gun`, `drop`, `none`, `hand`, `feed`) and open amounts of the moving parts. `mag` (АК-74, СВД, ВСС, Бизон, Кипарис): pull the magazine, a new one from the belt, rock it in, rack the bolt. `pistol` (ПМ): the magazine drops, a new one into the grip, the slide. `pump` (shotgun): four shells into the loading port, then the pump. `break` (ТОЗ-34): the barrels drop open, the cases fly out, two shells go into the chambers, the barrels snap shut. `revolver` (РШ-12): the cylinder swings out, the ejector throws five cases, five rounds one by one, the cylinder closes. `clip` (СКС): lock the bolt back, a stripper clip into the guide, thumb the rounds down, the clip flies off as the bolt closes. `belt` (ПКМ): lift the feed cover, unhook the box, hook a new one, lay the belt on the tray, slam the cover, charge. `rocket` (РПГ-7): a warhead from the back, pushed into the muzzle, the hammer cocked. Gunmen also reload between bursts.
- Fists: a guard stance after a punch, a left jab, a right cross with a hip turn and a front kick as the third blow; the knife slashes, thrusts and stabs; the grenade is wound up behind the head and thrown over the top.
- Jumps tuck the legs, long falls windmill the arms, landings bend the knees. Knock-downs and deaths fall backwards or forwards depending on the push, and the body lies in one of three sprawls; getting up goes through a kneel. Hands up for «ПОВЯЗАЛИ», dancing in the club.
- In a car the driver sits reclined with both hands on the turning steering wheel at ten to two: the rim runs through the curled fingers (wrist 8.5 cm from the rim along the hand, 3.4 cm to the palm side), the palm faces the wheel, the thumb lies along the rim toward the top, the knuckles face the driver. When the wheel is out of reach the driver leans forward just enough (`st.reach`, adjusted every frame until the elbows keep 3 cm of slack) and looks into the turn; passengers rest their hands on their knees; the seat height adapts to the roof (`N.seatRoot`), a dark footwell hides the feet of tall drivers; a bike rider leans forward with hands on the bars. Weapons are hidden in vehicles except while shooting from a car.

**Getting into a car** (`world.tryEnter`, `stepEnter`; the player's state is `p.enter = { v, side, ph, t }`): Tyoma runs to the nearest door (`go`), turns to the car and pulls the handle (`open`, the door swings on its hinge; a locked car only rattles the handle and shows «Машина заперта.»), pulls the driver out if there is one (`jack`: the driver is thrown out behind him, falls, then fights or flees; passengers flee through the other door), ducks into the seat (`in`), slides over to the wheel when he came through the passenger door (`slide`) and pulls the door shut as he starts the engine. Moving during `go` or `open` cancels. **Getting out** at a standstill (`beginExit`, `p.exit`): open the door from inside, swing the legs out and stand up, push the door shut; above 2.5 m/s he simply gets out, above 8 m/s he jumps and tumbles. Doors are world state (`v.door[2]` open amounts, `v.doorT` targets, `v.doorHold` auto-close timers; `door` events carry the open and slam sounds). Pedestrians who board or leave a car (militia, passengers, mission actors) are not animated in the simulation — `seat()` and `exitVehicle()` stay instant for the mission logic — but the scene shows them climbing in or out (`anim.board`, `anim.alight`) with the door opening, and `holdT` freezes their AI for half a second.

- **Throw aim.** Holding the left button with a grenade or a Molotov charges the throw (`p.charging` 0 to 1 in 0.83 s, power `N.throwPower` = 0.35 + 0.65 · charge; the G quick throw is `N.QUICK_THROW` = 0.8). While the button is held the player turns to the aim, the arm draws further back with the charge, the camera moves 0.85 m over the right shoulder and up so the arc is seen from the side, and every tick `World.planThrow` runs the same flight step the real projectile uses (`moveProj`: walls, cars, roofs, water, bounces) from the same launch (`throwLaunch`). The plan (`p.throwPlan`) holds the path, the bounce points, where the first landing is, the end point (where the grenade lies when the 3 s fuse runs out, or where the bottle breaks), the blast radius (7.5 m; for the Molotov the 3.2 m fire), the lethal radius for a 100 hp man (`R · (1 − 100 / dmg)` ≈ 4.4 m), whether it ends in water, and `hits` from `throwHits` with the same distance rules as `explode` and `stepFires` (`kill` when the damage at that distance covers health plus armour). Releasing throws with exactly the planned power; R cancels, keeps the grenade, and the button must be let go before the next windup. `N.Guns.aim` draws the arc (a thin line and moving dots, dimmer after the first bounce), rings on the bounces, the zone (disc, dotted edge that follows the ground, a low glowing wall, a red dotted lethal ring, a pulsing centre mark; blue when a bottle will go out in water, flashing red when the player stands inside) and arrows over people (red: dies, amber: hurt) and cars in the zone. The HUD (`HUD.throwMeter`) shows a 12-segment power bar right of the centre (green, yellow, red, flashing white at full power) with the G mark, the percentage, the distance to the end point, warnings «ПОТУХНЕТ В ВОДЕ», «УЙДЁТ ПОД ВОДУ», «ТЫ В ЗОНЕ!», the count of people and cars in the zone, how many die, and the hint «Отпусти — бросок · R — отмена». Settings «Траектория броска» (`settings.throwAid`, on by default) turns off the arc, the zone and the predictions; the power bar stays. Tests: section «throw aim»; screenshots with a `script` scene that holds `nnui.mouse.l`.
- **Rockets** are projectiles of type `rocket` (`stepRocket`): a raycast every step against peds, cars and the static world, a splash in water, and `explode(..., 'rocket')`.
- **Effects.** Rifles and pistols throw brass to the right (`WEAPONS[id].brass`), the pump and the break-open throw out shells, the РШ-12 its five cases; muzzle flash and tracer size come from `WEAPONS[id].fx`; the РПГ-7 adds back-blast smoke and a smoke trail; fires flicker, smoke and light the street; burning people carry flames and light. Every weapon has its own synthesized shot and reload sounds.
- **Who carries what.** Bandits carry the ПМ, ТОЗ-34, Кипарис or РШ-12, soldiers the АК-74 or ПКМ, the FSB the АК-74, ВСС or Бизон (`PED_KINDS[kind].arms`). The AI keeps its preferred distance per weapon (`aiRange`: 7 m with the ТОЗ-34, 26 m with the ВСС).
- **Shops and pick-ups.** `unlockWith` opens a weapon in the shop together with another one; the ТОЗ-34, СКС, Кипарис and the Molotov also lie on the streets.

## Water

Tyoma cannot swim. `city.waterAt(x, z)` returns `{ level, bed }` for raster water: the Oka has its surface at `N.WATER_Y` and a bed that follows the visible sand slope from the bank line (`N.BANK_SLOPE`, dry for the first four metres, waist deep at five, out of his depth from about seven), ponds have their surface at ground level and a bed half a metre deeper per metre from the shore. `groundY` returns the bed, so people and cars walk and drive down the bank into the water instead of walking on it.

- **Wading.** In water up to the chest everyone slows down with depth (down to a third), cannot jump, leaves drops and rings at every step and makes wading sounds; the first time the player wades, a help line warns that he cannot swim.
- **Drowning.** Deeper than 1.25 m (`STAND` in `stepWater`) a person loses footing: he floats chest deep, thrashes with both arms, the head thrown back, and can only paddle at 0.6 m/s. The player has 3.2 s (pedestrians 1.9–3.3 s) to reach water shallow enough to stand in; otherwise he goes under with a gulp and bubbles and dies after 1.3 s (`killPed(p, null, 'drown')`). Drowning ignores armour and god mode, it is not damage. The body sinks to the bed. After «ПОТРАЧЕНО» the hospital line starts with «Тёму выловили из воды и откачали».
- **Falling in** with speed splashes; deep water brakes the fall, so jumping off a bank or a pier never hurts — it drowns.
- **Pedestrians** controlled by the AI treat water deeper than 0.7 m as a wall and walk around; knocked into the river by a car or an explosion they drown like the player.
- **Cars** sink when the water reaches 0.45 m above their floor and settle on the bed; the player is pushed out of a sinking car into the water. Grenades splash, slow down and sink before they explode.
- The camera never dips under the surface; the death camera looks down at the rings where he went under.

## Weather

`nn-weather.js` (simulation, loaded before `nn-world.js`) owns the weather; `world.wx` is a `GtaNN.Weather` with its own random stream, so weather never shifts the AI's random numbers. `world.weather` reads the current kind, `world.setWeather(kind, mode, lock)` changes it (`'instant'`, `'fast'` over about three seconds, or the natural rate of about 45 seconds).

| Kind | Cover | Rain | Fog | Wind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `clear` «Ясно» | scattered cumulus | — | — | 2.5 m/s |
| `cloudy` «Пасмурно» | overcast | — | haze | 5 m/s |
| `rain` «Дождь» | storm clouds | 0.35–1 in bursts, thunderstorms | light | 7 m/s |
| `fog` «Туман» | thin | — | dense ground fog | calm |

- **Continuous state.** Cloud cover, darkness, haze, fog and wind move toward the preset; rain only starts once the sky is overcast, so a change from clear to rain plays as clouds first, then drops. Rain intensity changes in bursts every 25–80 s.
- **Wet streets.** `wet` rises within seconds of rain and dries in about four minutes in daylight; `puddle` fills over about a minute and dries in about ten. After the rain the asphalt stays wet and mirror-like for a while, and a rainbow (`rainbow`) appears if the sun is up and low (below about 30°).
- **Natural cycle.** A Markov chain picks the next kind when the timer (150–600 s) runs out: clear tends to cloud over, overcast to rain, rain clears through overcast. Fog is three times more likely at 4–9 in the morning and rare at midday. A natural change emits `{ t: 'weather', natural: true }`; in a car with the radio on, the DJ reads a forecast from `GtaNN.FORECAST` (`nn-story.js`).
- **Thunderstorms.** In heavy rain lightning strikes every 7–26 s at 0.5–4 km in a random direction with one to three flashes; the strike emits `{ t: 'thunder', delay: distance / 343 }` and the UI plays a crack and a rumble after that delay.
- **Gameplay.** `wx.grip` (down to 0.8 on a flooded road) scales tyre grip and braking in `Phys.stepVehicle`, so the braking distance grows. `wx.sight` (0.5 in fog, about 0.78 in rain) scales how far militia on foot (45 m) and in cars (70 m) see the player, so fog is a way to lose a wanted level. `wx.crowd` thins the pedestrians in rain. Missions are unaffected; the first mission forces a clear night (`setWeather('clear', 'instant')`).

## Rendering and shaders

All GLSL lives in `nn-shaders.js` (`GtaNN.GLSL`, WebGL2 / GLSL ES 3.00, shared chunks for integer hashing, value noise, fbm, fog and soft particles); `nn-render.js` compiles the programs and runs the frame. `GtaNN.envFor(clock, wx, draw, interior)` turns the time of day and the continuous weather into one environment: sky colours, sun and moon, fog distances, ground fog, light pollution, wetness, lightning, rainbow and the colour grade.

Static chunks are stored in world coordinates, which are kilometres from the origin. The world vertex shader subtracts the eye position first (`u_eyeL`, eye minus the draw origin) and projects with a rotation-only matrix (`renderer.vpEye`), so float32 rounding no longer moves sign faces, billboard ads and the layered road sign art against their plates from frame to frame (the old full matrix did, and they flickered while driving).

Plants use kind `FOLIAGE` (19). Byte 30 of the vertex is the sway amplitude in metres (0–1), byte 31 the fade distance in 2 m steps (grass only). The vertex shader bends foliage with the wind (`u_wind`, slow sway plus a fast leaf flutter) and pushes it away from up to four movers (`u_push[4]`: x, z, radius, top height; the player on foot, the player's car and the nearest moving cars, from `UI.pushers`), so grass and bushes part and flatten around whoever walks or drives through them while crowns above the mover's roof stay put. The fragment shader lights foliage with the vertex normal (crown-shaped normals, wrapped diffuse), adds translucency when the leaves are between the camera and the sun, skips grime, and dissolves grass with a screen-space dither before the end of the grass radius.

Fences, picket fences and railings use kind `DECAL` (alpha cut-out, normal lit and turned toward the viewer), not `LEAF`, so the wind sway for foliage does not bend them.

Frame order:

1. Opaque pass into a multisampled HDR target (RGBA16F with `EXT_color_buffer_float`, RGBA8 otherwise): backdrop, city chunks, road decals, cars and people.
2. Sky after the opaque pass, only where the depth is still clear.
3. Depth resolve, then the transparent pass that reads it: car glass, sprites with soft edges, procedural effects, GPU rain.
4. Colour resolve, bloom (bright pass and a four-level dual-filter blur), sun shafts, then the composite to the screen.

| Program | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `world` | Hemisphere, sun and up to twelve point lights. Per-layer surface table (`GtaNN.SURFACE`, a 256×1 texture: puddle, wet darkening, gloss, wet gloss): rain darkens porous surfaces and makes asphalt glossy; puddles from world-space noise with rain-drop ripples; anisotropic light streaks of lamps and headlights on wet roads; sky reflection with Fresnel; leaves sway with the wind; river waves scale with the wind and catch rain rings; height fog with sun in-scattering; lightning lights everything. Writes a reflection mask into alpha for screen-space reflections. |
| `sky` | Gradient with a Mie halo, sun disc with limb darkening, a phased moon, twinkling stars, domain-warped clouds lit from the sun side with silver linings, cirrus, the orange city glow under night clouds, lightning glow inside clouds, jagged bolts with branches, a double rainbow, haze toward the horizon. |
| `sprite` | Particles and ground decals; smoke is lit by the ambient light, soft edges where particles meet geometry. |
| `fx` | Procedural effects without textures: muzzle star and flame jet (shape per shot), moving tracers (pellets thinner), bullet impact flash, fireballs that turn into smoke, car fires, ground shock ring, volumetric beams of headlights and street lamps in fog and rain (slices when seen along the beam). |
| `rain` | 9 000 instanced streaks wrapped around the camera, slanted by wind and stretched by camera motion, lit by nearby lamps and lightning, plus ground splashes. |
| `bright`, `down`, `up`, `rays` | Bloom chain and sun shafts (radial blur of the sky toward the sun). |
| `comp` | Screen-space reflections on wet ground and water (view-space march with refinement, vertical blur on rough surfaces), screen-space rain sheets at three depths, raindrops on the lens, shock waves of explosions, heat haze over distant asphalt on clear afternoons, lightning exposure, hue-preserving tonemapping, per-weather grade (saturation, lift, gain), grey or blue fade on «ПОТРАЧЕНО» and «ПОВЯЗАЛИ», vignette, grain, dithering. |

Car lamps and plates. Lamps are geometry with the `lamplens` layer: round discs or rectangles in real sizes with a bezel, laid out per `shape.lights` (front) and per rear style (`classic`, `wide`, `round`). Lamp kinds 20–28 (`GtaNN.LAMPKIND`: head, tail, blue, red, taxi, reverse, left and right indicators, plate lamp) are resolved in `Dyn.add` from the per-car lamp state: lit lamps become emissive, unlit ones get the lens colour of their kind. `buildCar` returns `lamps`, one anchor per lamp group and side; the scene draws the glow sprites there, faded by the angle between the lamp and the camera (`GtaNN.lampGlow`) and pulled toward the camera, so a glare never shows through the cabin and the car's own bumper does not cut it. Headlight beams come from the same anchors and are drawn only in fog and rain. `v.rev` lights the reverse lamps; AI traffic sets `v.ind` (−1 left, 1 right) on the approach to a turn at a junction and 2 (hazard) while it recovers from a crash. Number plates are separate quads per car from the atlas (`plate|<class>|<i>`, the texts in `GtaNN.PLATES`): white civil plates «А 123 ВС 52», blue militia plates «А 1234 52», black army plates. Car glass is drawn back to front, with alpha from a Fresnel term (clear head-on, mirror-like at grazing angles).

Car bodies. `nn-cars.js` builds a body as a loft along the length of the car. Each model is data: curves of the rocker, lower crease, belt line, hood and trunk deck, roof and plan-view width as keys in metres behind the front axle (`a`), interpolated with monotone cubics; the windshield and back window as a base and a top position with corner offsets, so the glass wraps at the corners; wheel arches as circles; door lines, the window layout of the side glass (glass, vent bars, frames, B-pillar, the sloped edge of the rear door glass) and per-model front and rear faces written as small functions (grille, lamps, frames). A cross-section has ten points per side (rocker, door bottom, lower crease, shoulder crease, belt, window sill, glass top, roof rail, roof middle, roof centre); in front of and behind the cabin the upper points lie on the hood and trunk, so one topology covers the whole car. Every quad of the loft is classified per triangle (paint, glass, door, chrome or black trim) and the triangulation follows the boundary, which gives curved windshield edges and the slanted glass edge without extra geometry. Stations carry a mask: arch stations go only into the lower strips, window and windshield stations only into the upper ones. Front doors, their glass and their inner panel are separate meshes, cut along the real door lines; `shape.ax` (front overhang) and `shape.door` in `GtaNN.VEHICLES` put the doors the people use (`GtaNN.cabin`) at the same place. Wheels stand at the real axles and track; tyres, chrome hubcaps or painted steel wheels are lathed per model. Army at six stars. `N.DISPATCH[6]` units carry an optional `cap` (at most two БТР-80 and one Т-72Б alive at once); armour spawns with the `armor` vehicle AI (`AI.vmodes.armor`, `AI.armorDrive`, `AI.lineClear`): it drives along the road graph (`AI.pathTo`) until it is in range (БТР 40–80 m, tank 30–90 m), the БТР backs off when the player comes closer, the tank rams a stopped car; the turret leads the target (`aimTurret`), the AI checks the line of fire every 0.3 s (a raycast from the muzzle that must reach the player or his car, so no shots through buildings or other vehicles) and fires only after seeing the player for a moment (tank 1.5 s): the tank one shell every 6–9 s (`fireTurret(..., spreadK)` scatters AI shots), the БТР the КПВТ in bursts, both the ПКТ under 70 m. When it has no line of fire for six seconds it moves in closer. The Урал-4320 and the army UAZ chase like the OMON vans and unload their soldiers. Helicopters: `N.HELIS` (name, hp, bullet share `armor`, hit box `hit` [centre, half length, half width, y0, y1], the Mi-24 gun and rocket pods), `AI.newHeli(type, x, z, y)`; `DISPATCH[level].heli` names the type, and a helicopter of the wrong type leaves before the next one comes. The Mi-24 (`GtaNN.Army.mi24()`: tandem canopies, engine intakes and exhausts, stub wings with four rocket pods, the chin gun turret, a red star on the boom, five blades) circles 90 m out at 40 m (`AI.gunship`, mode `orbit`) and every 7–11 s makes a run: it hovers 55 m from the player at 26 m, nose on, fires the ЯкБ-12,7 (`VEH_GUNS.yakb`) in bursts and a salvo of four rockets from the pods with a lead, all only with line of sight. Helicopters can be shot: `World.raycast` returns `kind: 'heli'` (`heliRay`), bullets do `armor` × damage, blasts full damage (`damageHeli`, `explode`); at zero hp it falls spinning (`AI.fallHeli`), burns and explodes on the ground (`helicrash`). Sounds: the cannon (`cannon`), the КПВТ and ЯкБ (`kpvt`), the player's diesel pitch by type, and `Audio.updateMachines` loops for the nearest armour (diesel pulse, track clatter that speeds up with the tracks) and the nearest helicopter (rotor thump); sirens only sound from vehicles with a siren beacon.

Car materials. The last two bytes of every vertex (`a_lk.zw` in the world shader) carry a material pair: for car paint the gloss of that car, for glass its tint, and in the second byte the grime baked by `bakeWear` (low on the body and around the wheel arches, which also get darker vertex shading) times the dirt of that car. `Dyn.add(..., look)` writes them from `GtaNN.carLook(v)`: gloss falls with the typical build year of the model (`year` in `GtaNN.VEHICLES`) and varies per car, dirt grows with age (`dirty` adds for utility vehicles), the glass is tinted on the Mercedes, BMW and Land Cruiser and on some «девятки» (`tint`, `tintChance`); liveries set their own `gloss`, `dirt` and `tint` (the FSB Volga is polished black with dark glass, army vehicles are matte and muddy). A burnt car gets gloss 0 and the shader draws charred metal with rust patches. Paint is a clear coat over the base colour: Fresnel reflection (F0 0.1, cubic falloff) of `carEnv`, an environment made of the sky, a skyline band with lit windows at night and the ground, blurred by low gloss, plus sun and lamp highlights. New kinds: chrome (bumpers, trims, handles, mirror faces and lamp rims of cars with `chrome`), lens (unlit lamps: lit reflector plus a sharp glint) and trim (black plastic of the later cars). Glass adds a sun glint and gets darker and more opaque with its tint. Headlights are spot lights: the world shader takes up to `GtaNN.MAX_LIGHTS` (16) lights, and a light with `d` (axis) and `cos` (cone edge) in `u_pld` is a cone; `GtaNN.spotCars` gives cones to the player's car and the three nearest cars with lights on (only the player's on «простые» effects), the other cars keep the soft light pool sprite on the road.

Settings → «Эффекты»: «полные» (everything above) or «простые» (no bloom, reflections, shafts, rain sheets, lens drops or soft particles; half the rain). Settings → «Детализация машин» (`settings.cars`, presets in `GtaNN.CAR_DETAIL`): «высокая» (detail steps at 24 and 64 m, cabins of near cars, steering wheels of driven cars up to 60 m, three headlight cones besides the player's), «средняя» (16 and 44 m, steering wheels up to 30 m, two cones), «низкая» (9 and 28 m, only the player's car keeps its cabin, one cone); `GtaNN.carLod(distance, own, preset)` picks the level, and the player's car is always at full detail.

Vehicle drawing cost. Vehicles are transformed on the CPU into the dynamic buffer every frame, so their cost is the number of vertices `Dyn.add` writes. `Dyn.add` has a fast path for static meshes: the matrix sits in locals, colour and material words are copied whole unless the vertex is paint (one precomputed paint word, the car's gloss and dirt), a lamp, tinted glass or dimmed; skinned meshes (people) go through `Dyn.addRig`, and a test checks that both paths write the same bytes. Wheels were a third of all vehicle vertices: a far wheel (LOD2) is now a tyre band with a hub disc under 60 vertices, a middle one (LOD1) has 9 segments and no inner sidewall (under 170), and a far car draws all its wheels as one mesh baked when the model is built (`m.wheelsMb`), one `Dyn.add` instead of four to sixteen; near wheels reuse scratch matrices. On the fixed scenes this took the street from 68.8 to 55.8 thousand vertices and `scene.fill` from 2.5 to 1.7 ms, the army column from 89 to 65 thousand vertices and from 3.8 to 2.0 ms; random traffic at 17:00 is back to 0.3 ms by day and 0.7 ms at night with 8–9 thousand vertices, below the figures before the vehicles were rebuilt. Pitfalls: GLSL `pow` with a negative base is NaN (one NaN pixel turns into a white bloom blob with a black core), so clamp quad coordinates and square with multiplication; screen-space reflections assume an upward normal, so only horizontal surfaces write the mask.

## Metro

The Avtozavodskaya line of the Nizhny Novgorod metro as it ran in the summer of 2000, inside the district: Парк культуры → Кировская → Комсомольская → Автозаводская. The stations opened in 1987–1989; beyond Автозаводская the line goes on to Пролетарская, which belongs to a district that is not built yet, so trains turn back in a dead-end tunnel closed by «РЕМОНТНЫЕ РАБОТЫ», the way roads end at road-works barriers on the surface. The fare is a 5-rouble token.

### Design

- **Fantasy and aesthetic.** Going underground into a marble Soviet station, waiting under the interval clock, riding a blue 81-717 through a dark tunnel while the announcer reads «Осторожно, двери закрываются!». Sensation and fantasy first; the ride is a breather between missions (low intensity, no threats unless the player brings them).
- **Verbs.** Walk down, pay at the turnstile (or jump it), wait, board, sit (F), ride, get off, take an exit. Walking the tunnels is allowed and dangerous: a train kills whoever stands on the track.
- **Role in the loops.** Fast travel along проспект Ленина (about 3.2 km, four minutes end to end, similar to driving through traffic, so it is a choice, not a dominant route) and an escape route with a cost: militia stands at every turnstile line, more come down while Тёма has stars, and from four stars the entrances are shut. No helicopter or police car can follow underground, so a quiet ride out of sight lets the stars fade as usual.
- **Rules and numbers.**

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Fare | 5 р. per entry (`N.METRO.fare`), paid automatically when walking through a turnstile; free to leave, free to re-enter the paid side while still underground |
| No money | the turnstile stops him and a help line explains the jump |
| Fare dodging | jumping a turnstile or walking in through the exit passage: crime `fare` (heat 40, one star if a militiaman is within 45 m), the attendant shouts |
| Opening hours | 05:15–00:10 (real hours of the line); outside them the pavilion doors are locked, exits always work |
| Wanted level | 1–3 stars: entry allowed, metro militia arrest or shoot like street patrols, reinforcements come down every 9 s up to two per star (OMON from three stars); 4+ stars: «Вход в метро перекрыт милицией» |
| Trains | six 4-car trains (81-717 head cars, 81-714 middle cars), 80 km/h top speed, 1 m/s² acceleration and braking, 25 s stops, 30 s loading at the terminus, 20 s unloading and turning; a train every 134 s, a full cycle 806 s |
| Track | a train at more than 2.2 m/s kills a pedestrian on the track bed (damage, so god mode survives) and throws the body ahead; it sounds its whistle when the player is on the track 40–170 m ahead |

### Space

The metro lies in the interior space east of `GtaNN.INTERIOR_X` (like the club «Мираж»), along a straight line at z = `N.METRO.OZ`. The stretches between stations have the real distances between the OSM station points (1 330, 994 and 851 m); `city.outsidePos(x, z)` maps any point of the line back onto the real map for the radar, the city map, the F7 map and the hospital after «ПОТРАЧЕНО». Metrics (`N.METRO.P`): island platform 102 × 10 m at y 0, tracks 6.5 m off the axis with the track bed at −1.25 m, hall walls at 8.4 m, flat ceiling at 4.2 m (the vault of Парк культуры rises to 7 m), stairs from the platform ends up 4.6 m to the vestibules, which stand over the running tunnels, exit stairs up another 2.8 m to the landings with the door to the street.

Every metro pavilion on the street (`city.metroGates`, one per OSM subway entrance) is a teleport pair: the pavilion door leads to the landing of its own exit stair, and the vestibule an entrance belongs to is chosen by its side of the station along the line. Exits are ordered across the vestibule by their real side of the line, so the left exit leads to the left side of the street. Exit signs name the nearest cross street in the accusative («ВЫХОД НА УЛ. ЛОСКУТОВА», «ВЫХОД НА МОЛОДЁЖНЫЙ ПР.»); a second line names a landmark within 220 m («К ГОСТИНИЦЕ», «К ПАРКУ КУЛЬТУРЫ») or, when two exits would read the same, the compass side.

Floors are multi-level: `MetroLine.floorY(x, z, y)` returns the highest floor at most 0.8 m above the given height, so the vestibule and the tunnel under it are both walkable; `Phys.stepPed` and the camera pass the pedestrian height. Floor slabs under the platform, the vestibules and the track bed are colliders too, so bullets hit them. The platform body blocks a pedestrian on the track: climbing back needs a jump; a low gate at each platform end can be jumped to walk into the tunnel.

### Trains and timetable

The track is two polylines (`MetroLine.legs`): eastbound on the south track from the dead end beyond Парк культуры through a crossover to Автозаводская and into its dead end, westbound back through the other crossover. `MetroLine.schedule()` builds the cycle from moves with a trapezoid speed profile and stops; trains are the same schedule shifted by a sixth of the cycle, so they never meet (the test samples the whole cycle). `Metro.poseAll` places every car from its bogie points (cars swing through the crossovers), sets door opening, the next station and the lamp ends. The doors open only at a standstill along a platform, on the platform side.

Riding: inside a car the camera switches to first person (`Metro.eye()`, eye height standing or seated; the player model is not drawn, `cam.fp`) and back to the third-person camera once he steps out. X skips the ride (`Game.metroSkip` → `Metro.skip`): behind a short fade the whole metro moves on in time (`Metro.toff`, `Metro.now()`), so the train arrives at its next stop with the doors open, every train keeps its place in the timetable, everyone in the cars stays in their place, the clock advances by the skipped seconds (one game minute each) and the time counts as unseen for the wanted level. `Metro.carry` moves everybody inside a car with it (seated people are pinned to their seat), `Metro.collide` keeps them between the car walls and lets them through an open door only, and pushes people on the platform out of the car body. `Metro.floorY` adds the car floor at platform height. F inside a car sits on the nearest free seat (`Metro.act`); moving, jumping or F stands up.

### People

Passengers live only around the player (`Metro.stationLife`, `trainLife`): a station within 230 m is filled with waiting people (count by the hour, `Metro.crowd`), newcomers come down the exit stairs, through the turnstiles and onto the platform; trains approaching that station get riders (seated or standing, with a destination further along). At a stop riders whose station it is (all of them at a terminus) walk out through the nearest door and leave through an exit, and waiting people of the right direction walk to the nearest door and take a seat. Their AI is the pedestrian mode `metro` (`Metro.pedStep`); flee, fight and cop modes work as on the street and return to `metro` afterwards. A militiaman stands by the attendant's booth of every open vestibule.

### Drawing and sound

`nn-metrogfx.js`: `GtaNN.MetroGeo.build(geo)` builds the stations, vestibules, exit stairs, tunnels with cables, lamps and signals, and the dead ends with the crossovers into segments that the renderer culls by distance and frustum when the camera is underground (`renderer.metroVaos`); `GtaNN.MetroDraw.fill` draws the trains (body, cabin, glass, sliding door leaves, head and tail lamps from the lamp kinds, headlight cones in the tunnel), the interval clocks over the departure portals (current time and minutes:seconds since the last departure, from the atlas cells `mled`), departure and line signals, the daylight in the exit doors (dimmed at night) and picks the nearest station and tunnel lamps as point lights. `GtaNN.envFor(clock, wx, draw, 'metro', { k, tint })` is the underground environment: brightness `k` from `MetroLine.light` (dark in the tunnels, full in halls and vestibules and inside a car) and the station tint (sodium orange at Парк культуры); screen-space reflections stay on for the polished floors. New texture layers: `marble`, `lino`, `metromap` (the line scheme above the doors and in the vestibules), `mosaic`, `autopanel`; atlas styles `msign` (backlit metro signs) and `mled`. The metro lies about 22 km from the origin. There a float32 position has a 2 mm step and the GPU product of the view-projection matrix (translation about 22 000 m) with the position lost so much that vertices jittered by up to 2 px and depth by 5–10 cm at 10–20 m while the camera moved (people's heads, overlays and most surfaces flickered). Rendering is therefore origin-relative: the `world`, `sprite` and `fx` shaders take positions relative to `u_org` (`v_w = a_pos + u_org` stays the world position for lights and fog) and `Renderer.origin(u, org)` sets `u_vp` built from that origin in float64 (`vpAt`); city chunks use the origin 0 as before, every metro segment and the club are rebased to their own origin at upload (`GtaNN.rebaseMesh`), and the dynamic buffers (people, cars, trains, glass, sprites, effects) are written relative to the rounded camera position (`reset(org)` in `Scene.fill`). Large texture coordinates also jittered: `localUV` shifts the texture coordinates of every connected piece of the metro geometry by whole tiles so they stay small, and layered details (floor bands, frames, lamps, glass, clock digits) stand at least 1.5 cm off the surface under them; no two overlapping faces share a plane (culling is off, so opposite-facing walls in one plane fight too). Lighting is stable on purpose: station and vestibule lamps are emissive surfaces only, not point lights (picking the nearest of a hundred lamps every frame made highlights pop on and off as the camera moved), the halls get a brighter uniform environment instead; only the three nearest tunnel lamps in the camera's tube, car cabin lights and train headlights are point lights, and each fades out with distance before it is dropped. Station names are opaque plates (atlas style `mname`, no alpha test, which aliased at grazing angles), metro floors use the soft-grained `mgranite` layer instead of the speckled `granite`, the marble veins are soft, and screen-space reflections stay off underground (their stepped ray march shimmered on the polished floors).

Sounds (`nn-audio.js`): `updateMetro` mixes the rumble, the wheel beat on the rail joints and the tunnel hiss from `Metro.sound()`; one-shots `mdooropen`, `mdoorclose`, `mbrake`, `mhorn`, `mtoken`, `mslam`, `mwhistle`. Announcements are subtitles (`mtalk` events): inside the car «Станция «…»», «Осторожно, двери закрываются! Следующая станция — «…»», at a terminus «Конечная. Поезд дальше не идёт, просьба выйти из вагонов»; on a platform the closing announcement of a train at that station.

## Photo mode

F8 (or «Фотоаппарат» in the pause menu) stops the world and hands the player a camera of the summer of 2000. Everything lives in `nn-photo.js` (`GtaNN.Photo`, the data in `GtaNN.PHOTO`, storage in `GtaNN.PhotoAlbum`); the renderer, the scene, the audio, the pause menu and the game state have small hooks for it.

### Design

- **Fantasy.** Tyoma went to prison in 1992 and came out into a city he does not recognise. A cheap compact camera with an orange date in the corner lets the player keep that city as it looked in July 2000. It serves the pillar «Лето 2000 года»: the films, the camcorder, the date imprint, the print frames and the newspaper all belong to that year.
- **Aesthetic (MDA).** Expression first (framing, lens, film, print), discovery second (the newspaper's list leads the player to the district landmarks that were built from photos), sensation third (a stunt jump, a burning car, a lightning bolt or a tracer frozen in mid-air).
- **Verbs.** Freeze, fly the camera, frame, tilt, zoom, focus, pick a film and a print, shoot, sell, look back through the album.
- **Loops.** Moment: compose a frozen moment and press Enter; the shutter clicks, the frame flashes white and the print slides in at once. Session: a roll of 24 frames, each saved as a file the player can open on the Win 98 desktop. Meta: twelve views of the district for the newspaper «Автозаводец» (300 р. each, 3 000 р. and a 2001 calendar for all twelve), money that feeds the usual sinks, a photo as the desktop wallpaper.
- **What it does not do.** The camera takes the moment the city gives: it does not change the time or the weather (that stays in F7), does not pose Tyoma and records no video. It cannot leave Tyoma: the camera flies at most 40 m from him (14 m indoors), so photo mode is no scouting tool, and the newspaper views must be earned on foot.

### Viewfinder and panel

The screen has one job: show the picture that will be saved and the settings that make it. The panel stands at the right edge; the viewfinder is the free area left of it, so the panel never covers the picture. The frame (aspect and print) is fitted into that area, everything outside it is darkened, the print paper and the date are drawn exactly as they will be saved. The projection is shifted (`cam.shift`, the off-axis terms of the projection matrix) so the centre of the frame is the optical axis, and the lens angle is defined over the short side of the frame, not the screen: hiding the panel (H) makes the viewfinder bigger but does not change the picture. Over the picture: the focus point with the distance (white for auto, green when locked), an optional rule-of-thirds or horizon grid, the key line at the bottom and, at the edge of the 40 m sphere, «Дальше от Тёмы не отойти».

The panel has three groups of rows — «Камера» (lens, tilt, aperture, focus, exposure, flash), «Плёнка и печать» (film, frame, print, date) and «Видоискатель» (grid, Tyoma in the picture) — then «СНЯТЬ» and the small actions (hide, reset, album, exit). ↑/↓ choose a row, ←/→ change it; the mouse clicks ◄ ►, the wheel over a row changes it, the wheel over the picture changes the lens, a held mouse button turns the camera. Film, frame, print, date, grid, aperture, focus mode and Tyoma's visibility are remembered in `settings.photo`; lens, tilt and exposure start fresh each time (the lens nearest to the game camera's angle).

After a shot a print slides in at the bottom left for a few seconds: the thumbnail, the frame number and the file, and which newspaper views were in it.

### Rendering

- `Renderer.render(f)` takes `cam.up` (the tilted camera) and `cam.shift`; `f.photo` carries `dof`, `ev`, `film` and `flash` from `Photo.params(W, H)`.
- Depth of field is its own pass (`GLSL.dof`) between the bloom and the composite: a single-pass gather over a golden-angle spiral on the resolved HDR colour and depth, circle of confusion `c = f² · H / (N · s · 24000) · |z − s| / z` pixels (focal length f in mm, f-number N, focus s and depth z in metres, H the short side of the frame in pixels), capped at 15 px per 1000 px. Background samples cannot blur more than twice the centre's circle, so a sharp subject keeps its edge in front of a blurred background. The pass needs the resolved depth, so with «простые» effects it blits the depth anyway.
- Films are uniforms of the composite (`u_pf`, `u_pg`, `u_pts`, `u_pth`, `u_pvig`): saturation, monochrome, contrast, split toning, faded blacks, vignette and grain whose size follows the picture height; the camcorder adds line jitter and colour bleed before the scene is graded and scanlines after.
- Exposure multiplies the game's own exposure; the flash is one more point light in the scene's light list.
- Tyoma is left out by `cam.hidePlayer` in `Scene.fill` (on foot and in a car).

### Shooting and storage

`Photo.capture()` renders the frozen frame again at up to 2.5 times the canvas size (the long side of the picture reaches 1024 px), copies the frame out of the WebGL canvas in the same task, draws the print and the date on a 2D canvas and encodes a JPEG (quality 0.82, about 50–130 KB). The canvas is put back to its size and rendered again before the browser shows anything.

Photos are files: `C:\My Documents\Мои рисунки\Нижний Сити\Снимок_NNN.jpg` in the desktop's virtual file system, so Explorer, Paint and Imaging open them. The virtual file system lives in localStorage, so a shot is refused with a message when everything stored would pass 4.5 million characters. Metadata (zone, date, time, weather, lens, film, views) is kept in `localStorage.gtann_photos`. On the standalone page without a desktop the roll is kept in memory for the session.

The pause menu tab «Фотоальбом» shows the selected photo large, a film strip of the roll under it, the facts of the shot and three actions: «На рабочий стол» (B, the Win 98 wallpaper), «Открыть в программе» (O, Imaging) and «Удалить» (Del, asks once more, deletes without the Recycle Bin so the space really comes back). ←/→ browse. Under the actions is the newspaper's list of views with the ones already sold ticked. «Статистика» counts the photos and the views.

### Views for the newspaper

`GtaNN.photoSpots(city)` turns `PHOTO.VIEWS` into anchors on the map: Дворец культуры ГАЗ, кинотеатр «Мир», универмаг with its roof letters, гостиница «Автозаводская», Радиусный дом, главная проходная ГАЗ, Ленин у проходной, башня заводоуправления, трубы ГАЗа (the tallest striped chimney), ворота Автозаводского парка, колесо обозрения and the Мызинский мост. ## Story

### One page

- **Premise.** Summer 2000. Timofey «Tyoma» Vereshchagin walks out after eight years for a 1992 robbery and comes to Nizhny with $300 000 of Moscow money to buy forty «Volgas» off the books. The deal at a pier on the Oka is an ambush. To pay Moscow back he has to find the money in a city where every helpful friend has a share in the theft.
- **Theme.** Debts: who owes whom, and what silence is worth. Tyoma went to prison under the old rules (keep quiet, don't name your friends) and comes out into a city where friendship is a line in a ledger.
- **Arc.** Tyoma believes that keeping quiet is loyalty. In the finale he says out loud what happened in 1992, and Kesha, who ran away that night, stays and shoots.
- **Pillars.** Every mission adds a verb or twists one. Every contact wants something for what they give. The clue to the traitor is fair: the player can read it before Tyoma says it.
- **Structure.** Two threads braid and meet in the finale: Kesha → Лёва (m2, m4, m5, m7, m9) and the colonel (m3, m6, m8). One choice (the ledger in m7) changes the finale: who comes with Tyoma and what happens to Kesha.

### Backstory (1992)

Three friends from the Avtozavod: Tyoma, a GAZ test driver; Kesha, a law student; Лёва, a market hustler nicknamed «Шампанский» because he sold «Советское» poured into «Moët» bottles. Лёва planned to steal Volga engines from a GAZ warehouse for buyers in Kazan. Tyoma drove the truck, Kesha kept watch. The night watchman caught them, Лёва hit him with a pipe, Kesha ran. Tyoma stayed, called the ambulance, was arrested and named nobody. He got eight years for the theft and the beating. Лёва sold the engines anyway and built the club «Мираж» on that money. He sent two parcels in eight years.

## Missions, cutscenes and voice: the engine

Modules: `nn-cut.js` (cutscene runner and staging, SIM, after `nn-story.js`), `nn-missions.js` (mission runner, script helpers `GtaNN.MS`, taxi and militia jobs), `nn-act1.js`…`nn-act3.js` (missions 1–3, 4–7, 8–10, registered with `GtaNN.addMission`), `nn-voice.js` (Web Speech voice, GFX, after `nn-audio.js`). Characters, voice profiles and subtitle colours are in `nn-story.js`.

### Mission steps

A mission is `{ id, title, giver, marker, requires, reward, steps, fails?, club?, finale?, onPass }`. Step types:

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `call` | Runs `fn(run)` and moves on at once. |
| `cut` | A cutscene (see below). The world is frozen for the player, AI keeps moving. |
| `say` | Lines through the subtitle queue; with `wait: true` the step waits until they are spoken. |
| `talk` | A conversation that runs in the background while the next steps play. `need(run)` must hold for the next line (for example the passenger is in Tyoma's car); `away` is said once when it stops holding. |
| `goto`, `enter`, `kill`, `destroy`, `collect`, `lose`, `wait`, `choice` | As before. `goto` also takes `at: P(place, f, r)` or a function. |
| `fn` | Done when `fn(run, dt)` returns true; `bot(run)` lets the test bot finish it. |
| `tail` | Follow `tag` without coming closer than `near` for `spot` seconds in total (suspicion) and without falling back further than `far` for `lose` seconds. `spotted(run)` replaces the failure (m4 jumps to a chase step), `done(run)` ends it. |
| `cp` | A checkpoint: in play it only records the player and the mission variables; on retry `place(run)` rebuilds the world for this point and `kit` tops up weapons and armour. |
| `wanted` | Raises the wanted level. |

Any step can have `text`, `help`, `tick(run, dt)`, `fails`, `blips(run)` and `bar(run)` (a HUD bar such as «ГРУЗ» or «ПОДОЗРЕНИЕ»). `run.jump(id)` jumps to a step with that `id`. `GtaNN.MS` has the helpers the act files use: `P(place, f, r, yaw)` (offsets along the place's own yaw: `f` forward, `r` right), `at`, `npc`, `veh`, `crew`, `tp`, `seat`, `rm`, `give`, `dist`, `with`, `route`, `tags`, `count`, `hostile`.

### Failure, retry and the menu

`MissionRun.fail(text)` no longer cleans up. `Game.missionFailed` keeps `failInfo = { def, run, reason, cp, start, death }`: `start` is a snapshot of the player at the mission start (weapons, armour, money, outfit, flags, clock, weather), `cp` is the last checkpoint with its own snapshot and variables. While `failInfo` is open no other mission marker works.

- A plain failure shows «ЗАДАНИЕ ПРОВАЛЕНО» for about two seconds, then `failReq` opens the menu (`Menus.fail`).
- Death or arrest during a mission: «ПОТРАЧЕНО» / «ПОВЯЗАЛИ» plays, the screen fades to black and stays there (`fade.wait`); there is no hospital or militia station until the player chooses.

The menu offers «Повторить с контрольной точки» (only when one was reached), «Начать задание заново» and «Продолжить без задания». `Game.retry('cp' | 'start')` removes everything the failed run spawned, clears the wanted level, fires and stray cops, restores the snapshot (full health, the money and weapons of that moment, no hospital bill), and starts a new `MissionRun` from the checkpoint or from the first step. `Game.leaveFailed()` releases the run's people the old way (gangs attack) and, after a death or an arrest, does the usual respawn with the fee and lost weapons. Esc in the menu means «Продолжить без задания».

### Cutscenes

A `cut` step: `{ type: 'cut', at, shots, clear?, hide?, cast?, after? }`. `at` is the anchor frame: a place id, `'@tag'` (an entity), `[x, z, yaw]` or a function. `clear` removes passers-by around the anchor (default 14 m). Each shot: `{ cam, lines, beats, dur, pre, gap, hold, title, fade: 'in'|'out'|'both', slow }`.

Cameras:

| `cam` | Framing |
| --- | --- |
| `{ k: 'cu' / 'mcu' / 'ms' / 'fs', a, b? }` | Close-up, medium close-up, medium and full shot of `a`, from the side of `b` when given. |
| `{ k: 'ots', a, b }` | Over `b`'s shoulder onto `a`. |
| `{ k: 'two', a, b }` | Two-shot; the side is picked from where the two face. |
| `{ k: 'low' / 'pov' / 'top' / 'wide' / 'track', a, ... }` | Low angle, point of view, overhead, wide around an actor, a camera riding with a vehicle. |
| `{ p: [f, r, h], l: [f, r, h], p2?, l2? }` | Fixed position and target in the anchor frame. |

Options for all: `side`, `yaw`, `d`, `h`, `lh`, `fov`, a move `m: 'in'|'out'|'left'|'right'|'up'|'down'|'orbit'|'pan'|'zoom'` with amount `ma`, handheld shake `hand`. Every candidate is tested against buildings, fences and vehicles (camera inside, line of sight); the solver tries the other side, closer distances and other angles before it gives up, and an over-the-shoulder that cannot see falls back to a medium close-up. Actor shots follow the actor if he walks during the shot. At night a fill light rides with the camera; `S.light()` adds lamps for a scene.

Lines are `[who, text, opts]`; `opts` is a short string: a gesture (`'point'`), a target (`'>kesha'`: the speaker turns to Kesha and Kesha turns back), a channel (`'~phone'`, `'~radio'`, `'~pa'` shown as «(телефон)», «(рация)», «(динамик)» after the name). The speaker's head and arm move while the voice speaks (`anim.speak`).

Beats are `[time, fn(S, run)]`, `['L2', fn]` (when line 2 starts) or `['end', fn]`. The staging object `S` (`GtaNN.Stage`) has `walk`, `run`, `put`, `face`, `turn`, `gest`, `calm`, `aim`, `blank` (visual gunfire with tracers and sparks), `show`, `hide`, `seat`, `enter`, `exit`, `drive`, `park`, `brake`, `lamps`, `horn`, `door`, `boom`, `sfx`, `light`. A walk started in a shot snaps to its mark when the shot ends unless `keep: true`; skipping a line, a shot or the whole cutscene runs the remaining beats without their sound and light effects, so the world after a skip is the same as after watching.

Gestures (`anim.gest = { k, t, d }`, `GtaNN.GESTURES`): `point`, `wave`, `phone`, `give`, `beckon`, `hips`, `arms`, `shrug`, `calm`, `head`, `face`, `fist`, `count`, `back`, `drink`, `smoke`, `hug`, `duck`, `kneel`, `crouch`, `up`, `watch`, `nod`, `no`, `down`, `look`. They are hand targets in the chest frame solved with the arm IK, plus head and leg poses; a gesture that uses the hands hides the weapon. `look.hat = 'mask'` is a balaclava with an eye slit (the gunmen at the pier).

Controls: Space, Enter or a click ends the current line (after the last line, the shot); X skips the whole cutscene. The letterbox slides in and out; the first shot shows a hint.

### Voice

`GtaNN.Voice` speaks every subtitle line with the Web Speech API. Each character has a profile `{ g, pitch, rate, nat }`; `nat: true` prefers a natural online voice (Edge: «Дмитрий», «Светлана»), the others use the local voices (Windows: Pavel, Irina) where pitch works, so the cast stays tellable apart with only two base voices. A female voice used for a male part is lowered and the other way round. `GtaNN.voiceText` spells out abbreviations (СВД, ПМ, ГАЗ, ОМОН…) and drops quotes. A line lasts until its voice ends (with a cap), or for its reading time when there is no voice; a voice that does not start within 1.5 s falls back to the reading time. Street barks and the metro announcer are voiced too but never interrupt story lines. The radio ducks while someone speaks; pausing stops the voice and the line starts again after the pause. Settings: «Громкость голосов», «Озвучка реплик». The master volume reaches the voice through `SystemMixer`.

### Debug and tests

F7 «Задания» starts any mission (earlier ones are marked done), jumps to any checkpoint of the running mission, passes or fails it, and sets the ledger choice. Test sections «missions» (data, every line has a voiced speaker, the full chain for both ledger choices), «mission checkpoints» (every checkpoint of every mission is failed, retried and finished, then the mission is restarted), «mission failure menu» (death, arrest and a plain failure, retry and continue), «cutscenes and voice» (every intro camera is finite, skipping applies the staging, voice timing with a stub voice, gestures keep the rig finite). Shots: `cut:<id>[:1]` saves every shot of a mission (`:1` takes the ledger-to-Kesha branch), `failmenu`, `faildead`, `cargo`, `tail`, `radio`, `debugmissions`.

## Generative art: «Заречный закат»

The loading screen and the logo come from one seeded algorithm in `nn-art.js` (`GtaNN.Art.poster(canvas, seed)`, `GtaNN.Art.logo(canvas)`). Every seed is a different evening over the same city.

The picture is built as horizontal strata, the way the left bank of the Oka is seen from the water: sky, the far bank, the river, the panel districts, the foreground. Each stratum is a small process, not a drawing. The sky is a three-stop gradient picked from four palettes (pink dusk, violet twilight, night, golden evening). Cloud bands are thin polygons whose edges follow a sine plus value noise, so they drift in layers instead of repeating.

The far bank is a ridge line from fractal noise, with the Kremlin wall as a row of crenellations and four towers, and the TV tower as a thin vertical. It is tinted toward the horizon colour for atmospheric depth. The river takes the horizon colour and carries a glitter path: short strokes scattered around the sun's x, widening with distance from the horizon.

The districts grow from left to right in three depth layers. Each building is a rectangle whose width and height come from the layer scale; a quarter of them are towers. Windows are a grid, and a window is lit when a noise field over the whole image is above a threshold. Light therefore spreads in organic clusters across neighbouring buildings, the way whole stairwells and floors light up in the evening, instead of random dots.

GAZ chimneys are striped stacks with a red beacon. Their smoke is traced by particles moving through a curl-noise field (the rotated gradient of fractal noise), drawn as long translucent strokes, so plumes bend and braid without ever crossing themselves. The foreground adds poplar crowns made from clouds of dark ellipses, a street lamp with a warm halo and a Volga silhouette.

The last pass treats the image like a VHS frame from 2000: a vignette, a scanline every third row and per-pixel grain from a hash of the pixel and the seed. The same seed always gives the same picture.

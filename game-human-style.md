# GAME HUMAN STYLE

Rewrite existing game text so it feels human-written while preserving meaning, lore, character intent, gameplay information, terminology, names, variables and formatting.

Applies to dialogue, narration, lore, notes, item descriptions and quest text.

## Rules

- Prefer zero em dashes `—`. Maximum one when genuinely necessary. Rewrite the sentence instead of replacing it mechanically.
- Remove AI-like phrasing, excessive transitions, repeated conclusions and constructions such as `not X, but Y`.
- Do not explain a point twice or summarize what the player already understood.
- Vary sentence length and rhythm naturally. Do not make every paragraph equally polished.
- Avoid generic poetic language, fake profundity, unnecessary metaphors and stock bodily reactions.
- Never invent lore, motivations, dates, facts or character history.

### Dialogue

Characters should sound distinct and speak according to their personality, relationship, knowledge and current situation.

Dialogue is interaction, not alternating essays. Characters may answer briefly, interrupt, evade, lie, misunderstand, joke or leave things unsaid when appropriate.

Avoid exposition between characters who already know the information.

Do not make every line witty, dramatic or quotable.

### Lore and documents

Write according to the fictional author and purpose of the text. Prefer concrete facts over vague grandeur. In-world documents do not exist solely to explain the setting to the player.

### Gameplay text

Quest objectives, UI text and mechanical information must remain clear and precise.

Before output, silently check: facts preserved, no invented lore, voices preserved, unnecessary explanations removed, technical markers unchanged, em dashes minimized.

Return only the rewritten text unless analysis is requested.

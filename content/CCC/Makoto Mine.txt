Working Name 
Makoto Mine
Color Theme 
Red
Role 
Dimension Hopping Master Thief (pretending to be a transfer student)
Background 
In an alternate dimension, Jacob Kirby has become a pirate lord and sails the cosmos along with a harem of adoring women. Makoto is one of the many low status women who serve him and love him. As part of an attempt to impress him and rise to be his foremost concubine, she traveled in a dimension hopping device to our world and has been collecting alien treasures. Meanwhile, she’s joined Jacob’’s college in order to get close to this world’s version of him. Her theft of an alien treasure kickstarts the events of the story.
Personality
 Mischievous, sexually forward, and occasionally boastful. She hides her master thief persona behind a playful persona. Ultimately, what she really wants more than anything is to prove her worth to Jacob. However, this world’s version of Jacob doesn’t impress her enough to kick in this approval junkie mode at the start of the story.
Core Themes 
A desire to use escapism as a means to live a life that the normal world cannot provide for you normally. Building your happiness where it otherwise wouldn’t exist.
Inspiration 
French sensual comics. Anime and manga such as Lupin the Third and Mouse. Hentai such as Bible Black and Soukou Kijo Iris. Also elements of the western super hero tradition such as Mister Miracle. Supervillains like Catwoman and Black Cat.

However, as familiarity breeds and her respect for you rises, it becomes clear that she’s really looking to be topped and controlled during sex.

Working Name: 

Amanda Miller

Color Theme: 

Green (normal)/Pink (magical girl) 

Role: 

Magical Girl Defender of Earth. Also, Childhood friend to Jacob.

Background: 

Amanda Miller grew up as a normal American girl in a normal American suburb. She has issues with her own femininity, stemming from the fact that her father beat her mother when she was younger. As a result she views femininity as weakness. Her tomboyishness and inclination towards nerdy things led her to fall in with Jacob in middle school and the two remain friends. Five years before the events of the story, she rescued a strange bear-like creature from a crash site. This creature, a bog-o-bog, told her that he was here to select the new defender of earth from evil. The always justice inclined Amanda was thrilled...until it turned her into a sparkly pink magical girl. She hates her magical girl form, but whenever she hears the call, she reluctantly transforms into her other persona and battles whatever monstrosities are attacking from space this week. All while keeping it hidden from her family and Jacob.

Personality:

Amanda is abrasive and prone to sarcasm or biting remarks. Her relationship with Jacob is playfully antagonistic, but there is obvious care and affection between them. Indeed, it even betrays a small crush on him. The way she talks, the way she moves, the way she does everything is rather utilitarian. However, a bit part of her behaviour also stems from a fear of being perceived as too weak or feminine. In her magical girl form however, she is prone to embarrassment and her normal confidence is shattered. However, she still has a strange tendency to shout out attack names and talk in dramatic “magical girl-esque” pronouncements.

Core Themes: 

Repression of identity. The way fiction allows us to find a sense of self that we otherwise might normally accept. The way that community and finding other people to lean on helps us survive the problems in our own lives.

Inspiration: 

Magical girl stories of all types. Most notably however, Madoka Magika, Sailor Moon, Yuki Yuna is a Hero, and Precure. The comedic stylings are also familiar to Cutie High Defence Force Love!

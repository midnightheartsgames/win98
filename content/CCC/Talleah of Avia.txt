Working Name: 

Talleah of Avia

Color Theme: 

Blue

Role: 

Alien Princess

Background: 

The third daughter of the queen Yllanna of Avia. She was raised with the intention of her taking the role of Justicar in her older sister's regime. Avia is a quiet and peaceful planet, unique in that it does not have men. All reproduction is done technologically, though the Avian people once had men and thus possess the capacity for reproduction via more traditional means. While she is quite well trained and ruthlessly carries out orders, as per the customs of Avia, she is quite kind and hospitable to those who she is not tracking. As the guardian of the royal palace, she was the one at fault with the loss of her planet's treasure and thus the one who has been following the signature to earth. Here she has one mission. Bring back the lost treasure and the one who took in an attempt to restore her honor and regain her position as the future justicar of her people.

Personality:

(Name) is polite and amiable, raised as a princess on a planet with no war. However, when confronted with injustice she is easily angered, and when hunting a target she proceeds without hesitation or mercy. For all her formal prestige, she is actually quite inexperienced. She views the importance of her role as a princess highly, and the risk of censure and public shame if she fails her mission is always quite high on her mind.

She's never been off-world before, so she's quite fascinated about seemingly everything to do with planet earth. She's not dumb, but she lacks the world weariness you'd expect in someone her age. Thus, she's capable of applying her knowledge of her own culture to earth customs, and isn't horrible at blending into a crowd, but she does easily get tricked by things humans never would. She also has never met men before outside of a diplomatic setting, and she is quite surprised and curious about her feelings towards Jack. 

Deep down, she has something of an otaku's heart. Her own home world does have fiction and stories, and while they're much tamer, she always enjoyed them. So as soon as she starts getting the concept of nerd culture she takes to it quite easily. She especially enjoys soaring adventure stories.

Core Themes: 

Fiction as discovery of new worlds. An opportunity to see ways of living and corners of the universe you otherwise wouldn't have been able to imagine.

Inspiration: 

Western "B Sci-Fi" in general. 70s and 80s sci-fi anthology comics like Heavy Metal. 1950s sci-fi film serials like the Day the Earth Stood Still and Flash Gordon. 70s and 80s sci-fi infused super hero comics like the Chris Claremont X-men run, Jim Starling space sagas, and Marv Wolfman's run on Teen Titans. Manga and Anime from the late 80s and early 80s with a sci-fi bent such as Tenshi Muyo and Urusei Yatsura. 

Sexual Profile: 

While she has had sexual experiences before in her life, they've always been with other women and they've never been truly romantic. However, this is her first time with a man and she's quite inexperienced with physical sex. Just because she's new to it doesn't mean she isn't eager, and she's quite willing to experiment with the possibilities arrayed by the presence of a penis.

Physical Appearance: 

While in her Space Outfit/naked her skin has a decidedly light blue tint and her hair is a sheen of silvery white. She looks approximately of age with the other characters in the series (about college). Her figure is tall but not particularly voluptuous. She has small perky breasts and a dainty figure.

The way she blends in on Earth is via a technology that scans nearby people and creates outfits that match theirs. Thus, while infiltrating a cosplay convention, she will switch into the outfits of people already there. It will also change her skin and hair color to a tone that matches the scanned individual. The outfits changed into includes wigs, so both her Starfire and Ryoko outfits would include wigs as opposed to real hair.

Her most defining feature is her bright expressive eyes. They're very sympathetic and they contrast heavily with her role as a harsh pursuer of intergalactic justice.

When outside of cosplay, she wears an alien skinsuit that covers her torso but not her legs or her arms, much like a human swimsuit. The design has a diffrent texture and color over the mid section, and in that regard looks something like a plugsuit from Rebuild of Evangellion movies. She also wears a hear harness that slots in the back of her head undeerneath her hair with some sections wrapping around to the front. On her arms, she wears metalic bands on her forearms. She also has a pair of short boots on her feet (though those will only be visible in CG).
